STRATEGIC OVERVIEW

Engineered for Seamless Operations & Measurable Outcomes

Third-party logistics providers (3PLs), freight carriers, and distribution enterprises face escalating fuel costs, driver shortages, warehouse dock congestion, and customer demands for real-time shipment transparency.

Nova Arc develops mission-critical technology solutions for logistics and supply chain operations. We build Transportation Management Systems (TMS), dynamic route optimization engines, warehouse appointment scheduling platforms, and freight visibility portals.

By connecting GPS fleet telematics, warehouse picking queues, and carrier EDI networks, our platforms lower transportation overhead, reduce truck detention penalties, and ensure freight arrives safely and on schedule.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical guidance regarding project scope, integrations, and operational requirements.

How does the route optimization software account for commercial truck regulations? +
Our algorithms utilize commercial truck mapping profiles that factor in legal gross vehicle weight, bridge clearance heights, truck-restricted parkways, and mandatory driver Hours of Service (HOS) rest breaks.
Can shippers share live tracking links with their customers? +
Yes. When a shipment is dispatched, the system generates an encrypted live tracking link that can be emailed or texted to customers, showing the vehicle's progress and updated ETA on an interactive map.
How does dock scheduling reduce warehouse labor costs? +
By scheduling truck arrivals across balanced time slots, warehouse managers can align staffing schedules with actual incoming pallet volume, eliminating idle dock time and avoiding costly overtime spikes.
What happens when drivers lose cellular reception in remote delivery locations? +
Our mobile driver app operates offline-first using local device storage. Drivers capture signatures, scan barcodes, and photograph cargo without network connectivity; records synchronize automatically once cellular reception resumes.
How does electronic proof of delivery accelerate client invoicing? +
Completed ePOD records upload immediately upon customer signature capture, automatically attaching digital receipts to ERP invoices and initiating billing workflows within minutes of handoff.
Can the platform integrate with commercial freight visibility networks? +
Yes. We maintain native API connectors for project44, FourKites, and MacroPoint, along with standard EDI 204, 214, and 210 transaction sets, giving shippers unified multi-modal visibility.
