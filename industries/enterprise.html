FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical guidance regarding project scope, integrations, and operational requirements.

How does the system enforce confidential boundaries between competing client accounts? +
Our platforms incorporate project-level access controls and ethical wall configurations, ensuring only authorized team members assigned to a specific client matter can access that project's files, communications, and billing rates.
Can managers approve employee expense reports on mobile phones? +
Yes. Managers receive notification cards with attached receipt photos and expense line items, enabling one-click mobile approval or rejection with notes.
Can we customize the approval thresholds for different offices or business units? +
Yes. Approval hierarchies can be configured per legal entity, regional office, practice group, or project type, supporting custom expenditure tiers and currency rules.
How does the platform track consultant billable utilization and project margins in real time? +
By correlating logged timesheet hours against engagement fee structures and project expenses, executive dashboards calculate real-time gross margins per matter and highlight scope creep before budget overruns occur.
What safeguards ensure that historical billing and client records remain tamper-proof? +
We maintain immutable audit logs that record all timesheet submissions, expense approvals, invoice generations, and rate adjustments with cryptographic timestamps to satisfy rigorous financial audits.
How smoothly does the platform integrate with corporate Single Sign-On (SSO)? +
We provide native integration with SAML 2.0, Okta, Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD), and Ping Identity, supporting SCIM user provisioning so employee departures immediately suspend system access.
