STRATEGIC OVERVIEW

Engineered for Seamless Operations & Measurable Outcomes

Modern manufacturing and heavy industrial plants operate under demanding production schedules where unexpected equipment downtime and quality defects can cost millions of dollars in lost throughput.

Nova Arc engineers industrial-grade technology solutions for manufacturing plants and processing facilities. We deploy non-invasive IoT telemetry sensors, real-time Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) dashboards, and automated incident escalation workflows.

Our platforms connect factory-floor machinery, programmable logic controllers (PLCs), and maintenance management systems into a cohesive operational intelligence layer, enabling proactive maintenance and peak operational availability.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical guidance regarding project scope, integrations, and operational requirements.

Can you collect data from 20-year-old machinery without network connectivity? +
Yes. We install non-invasive external IoT sensors (current clamps on main power feeds, surface vibration sensors, external temperature probes) connected to an industrial edge gateway that transmits data over local Wi-Fi or cellular.
How does the system calculate Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)? +
The platform automatically tracks Availability (actual operating time vs planned run time), Performance (actual units produced vs theoretical maximum cycle speed), and Quality (good units produced vs total units) to compute live OEE for each line.
Does connecting IoT sensors to our plant floor create cybersecurity risks? +
No. Our edge gateways operate on an isolated subnet with no incoming listening ports. Telemetry is transmitted outbound over encrypted TLS channels using mutual certificate authentication.
How does the platform eliminate false alarms and nuisance alerts? +
We implement multi-condition validation and persistence windows. An alert is only triggered when sensor readings breach dynamic statistical limits for a sustained duration or across multiple correlated variables (such as simultaneous rise in motor current and vibration).
What CMMS platforms can automatically receive maintenance dispatch tickets? +
Our integration layer features certified bidirectional connectors for IBM Maximo, SAP Plant Maintenance (PM), UpKeep, MaintainX, and custom SQL databases, attaching telemetry wave graphs directly to work orders.
Can executive leadership benchmark productivity across multiple factory locations? +
Yes. Our enterprise dashboards standardize operational metrics across different plant facilities, providing normalized OEE comparisons, downtime category breakdowns, and machine availability rankings in real time.
