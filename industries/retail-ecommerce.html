PROBLEM & RESOLUTION

Operational Challenges & Technical Resolutions

How Nova Arc overcomes systemic process inefficiencies, operational friction, and technology bottlenecks.

Business Challenge

Business Problem

Checkout Latency & High Cart Abandonment

Why It Matters:

Bloated checkout funnels and slow load times frustrate mobile shoppers, causing lost retail revenue.

Proposed Resolution: We engineer streamlined, microservices-driven checkout experiences with tokenized payment processing and instant address lookup.
Business Challenge

Business Problem

Overselling & Out-of-Stock Cancellations

Why It Matters:

Selling items online that are physically out of stock in warehouses leads to cancelled orders and angry customers.

Proposed Resolution: Our platforms enforce sub-second inventory reservation at checkout, ensuring items displayed as available are physically in stock.
Business Challenge

Business Problem

High Return & Exchange Costs

Why It Matters:

Manual returns processing drains customer service bandwidth and delays restocking returned merchandise.

Proposed Resolution: We implement self-service customer returns portals that generate prepaid return labels and initiate exchanges automatically.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical guidance regarding project scope, integrations, and operational requirements.

How does the system prevent overselling when traffic spikes during a sale? +
When a buyer enters the checkout funnel, the system places a temporary 10-minute inventory reservation on the item. If the purchase completes, the stock is permanently allocated; if the session expires, the reservation releases back to available inventory.
Can B2B buyers see different pricing from retail consumer shoppers? +
Yes. When authenticated B2B accounts log in, the catalog dynamically switches to display negotiated contract pricing, bulk quantity tiers, and wholesale tax rules.
Does the platform support international currencies and localized taxes? +
Yes. Our platforms support multi-currency checkouts with automated geolocation, localized address forms, and real-time regional sales tax calculation via Avalara or Vertex.
How does intelligent order routing select the optimal warehouse for fulfillment? +
Our fulfillment engine analyzes customer shipping address, line-item availability across regional warehouses, carrier freight rates, and shipping cutoff times to route orders to the lowest-cost, fastest delivery facility.
How are customer returns and exchanges managed to reduce support workload? +
We deploy self-service customer returns portals where buyers input order numbers, select return reasons, generate prepaid carrier shipping labels, and initiate instant replacement orders without manual customer service calls.
Can physical store associates fulfill online orders for in-store pickup (BOPIS)? +
Yes. We build mobile-optimized store associate portals that receive pick-and-pack alerts for online orders, allowing store staff to scan items from retail shelves and stage them for customer curbside or counter pickup.
