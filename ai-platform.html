Continuous Autonomous Operations

What It Automates

Intelligent Authorization

Clinical NLP agents that replace manual payer requirement checking, compile clinical evidence, and track authorization determinations to slash turnaround time and eliminate missing-auth denials.

Sub-24h Authorization Determination

What It Automates

This capability replaces the labor-intensive manual routine of checking insurer-specific prior-auth rules, gathering clinical progress notes, and logging into fragmented payer portals. By automating auth submissions and status tracking, it drastically shortens authorization turnaround time and eliminates CARC 197 missing-auth denials. Patient access coordinators maintain active oversight, reviewing complex authorizations and managing clinical escalations.

  • 1
    Clinical Chart Extraction: Clinical NLP scans progress notes to locate proof of conservative therapy, physical therapy duration, and medication history.
  • 2
    Payer Policy Cross-Reference: Matches proposed procedures (CPT) against live clinical policy bulletins for UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, Cigna, and Medicare.
  • 3
    Automated Dispatch & Clinical Oversight: Transmits auth packets via payer gateways and tracks status hourly, while clinical intake specialists oversee peer-to-peer and denial reviews.

Core Capability 03

Autonomous Coding

Specialized clinical transformer models that assist chart review and suggest ICD-10 and CPT codes to surge coder productivity and shrink DNFB, operating under mandatory human-in-the-loop validation.

Sub-4-Minute Chart Coding SLA

What It Automates

Designed to assist professional medical coders, our clinical NLP interprets complex unstructured clinical notes, operative reports, and pathology findings to suggest accurate ICD-10-CM/PCS, CPT, and HCPCS codes. This capability dramatically accelerates coder productivity and reduces Discharged Not Final Billed (DNFB) days. To ensure compliance in this sensitive domain, human oversight is mandatory: certified coders review and confirm all AI-suggested codes prior to final submission.

  • 1
    Unstructured Clinical Prose Ingestion: Reads physician dictations, operative notes, pathology findings, and emergency triage summaries.
  • 2
    Autonomous Code & Modifier Assignment: Identifies primary diagnoses, secondary comorbidities (CC/MCC), surgical CPTs, and applies NCCI edit logic.
  • 3
    Human-in-the-Loop Validation: Rather than unsupervised auto-billing, certified AAPC and AHIMA coders review AI-suggested code sets, verify medical documentation, and approve final claims.
