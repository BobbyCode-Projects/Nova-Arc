Aging Stagnation

The Silent Bleed of Unworked Aged Receivables

In medical practices and specialty clinics, billing staff are consumed by daily claim submission, leaving aged accounts to languish until statutory timely filing deadlines permanently eliminate cash recovery.

EXPOSURE 01

The 90+ Day Aging Black Hole & Timely Filing Drops

As claims age past 60 and 90 days, collectability drops exponentially. Without automated multi-factor prioritization, billing clerks cherry-pick easy low-dollar claims while high-value claims slip past payer timely filing deadlines (CARC 29), resulting in catastrophic, irreversible bad debt write-offs.

Financial Risk: Over 25% of Practice Cash Stalled in 90+ Day Aging
EXPOSURE 02

Payer "Pending" Traps & Wasted Manual Call Time

Insurers frequently leave clean claims in "pending additional documentation" or "in review" status without issuing an official 835 denial. In-house billers waste 45+ minutes on hold per call just to hear scripted status responses, while practice days in A/R swell uncontrollably.

Operational Risk: 60+ Staff Hours Wasted Weekly on Routine Payer Status Calls
EXPOSURE 03

Silent Contractual Underpayments & Short Pays

Payers quietly short-pay physician claims by applying incorrect fee schedules, miscalculating multiple procedure payment reductions (MPPR), or ignoring bilateral surgical modifiers. Because a partial payment was received, the claim closes unnoticed, leaking 3% to 7% of net clinic revenue.

Revenue Leak: $75,000 to $180,000 Annually per Physician in Unclaimed Variances

Dedicated aged A/R task forces systematically sweep 30, 60, 90, and 120+ day buckets. Every claim receives definitive disposition—cleared for payment, appealed with evidence, or rebilled with corrections—driving > 90-day A/R below 9.5%.

• Bucket-Specific Resolution Playbooks
• Sub-30-Day Liquidation Velocity

4. Contractual Fee Schedule Underpayment Auditing

Automated remittance audits compare every paid line item against your practice's contracted commercial fee schedules. Underpayment variances trigger immediate automated demand letters and re-adjudication requests to reclaim short-paid revenue.

• Line-Item Contract Rate Verification

The 32% reduction in Days in A/R (DAR) is achieved through a combination of algorithmic work-queue scoring and automated electronic status interrogation (EDI 276/277). Rather than working claims in chronological or alphabetical order, our system scores outstanding claims daily by cash value, payer timely filing countdown, and collectibility velocity. By resolving high-dollar claims in the 30–60 day window and deploying robotic process automation (RPA) to clear "pending" payer queues without manual phone holds, the average receivable lifecycle compresses from 48.2 days to 27.4 days within 90 days of onboarding.

Nova Arc utilizes a proprietary multi-factor priority algorithm: Priority Score = (Balance × 35%) + (Timely Filing Urgency × 30%) + (Payer Velocity Index × 20%) + (Aging Multiplier × 15%). Claims approaching insurer timely filing cutoffs (e.g., 90 or 120-day commercial deadlines) automatically receive top priority to prevent forfeiture. High-dollar surgical claims and identified contractual underpayment variances are immediately assigned to senior billing specialists, while routine status inquiries are resolved electronically.

Many practices assume that if an insurer paid a claim, the payment was accurate. However, commercial payers routinely short-pay claims by 3% to 7% due to misapplied fee schedule updates, improper downcoding, or flawed Multiple Procedure Payment Reduction (MPPR) calculations. Nova Arc loads your practice’s contracted commercial fee schedules into our auditing engine. Every electronic remittance (835) is matched line-by-line against your contract rate. When a variance is detected, the system auto-generates a formal contractual underpayment appeal with fee schedule citations, recapturing short-paid funds.
