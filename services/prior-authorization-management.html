Operational Friction

The High Cost of Prior-Auth Gridlock

Prior authorization has become the single greatest operational roadblock for procedural facilities. Here is why manual pre-certifications erode surgical margin.

PROBLEM 01

OR Cancellations & Block Time Atrophy

When a commercial payer takes 10 to 14 days to approve an arthroscopy or spinal decompression, the surgical suite sits empty. ASCs forfeit $1,800 to $2,500 per lost operating room hour when unapproved cases are cancelled 24 hours before surgery.

Impact: 12% to 15% Annual Operating Margin Degradation
PROBLEM 02

Portal Fragmentation & Administrative Churn

Surgical coordinators toggle across 15+ different payer portals (Availity, CoverMyMeds, Optum, eviCore, AIM), manually re-typing operative indications and uploading PDF packets. Clinical staff spend an average of 2.4 hours per surgical authorization on administrative typing.

Impact: Staff Burnout & High Intake Turnover
PROBLEM 03

Fatal CARC 197 Denials & Unapproved Add-Ons

Surgeons perform necessary add-on procedures during surgery (e.g., unexpected meniscectomy during ligament reconstruction) without prior pre-certification. Payers deny the facility fee under CARC 197 (Precertification absent), turning thousands in implant costs into total write-offs.

Impact: Permanent Administrative Write-Offs

Robotic Process Intelligence

Payer Portal Automation Explainer

Not all health plans accept direct ANSI 278 EDI transmissions. Here is how Nova Arc's intelligent portal robotics automate the 15+ proprietary commercial payer gateways.

Automated 5-Step Portal Execution Sequence

  • 1
    Clinical EMR Extraction & Chart Scrubbing: Specialized healthcare NLP agents parse provider clinical notes, operative plans, radiology MRI/CT narratives, and conservative treatment timelines directly from your EHR without manual copy-pasting.
  • 2
    Payer Clinical Guideline Crosswalking: Algorithms match the extracted documentation against specific payer medical necessity criteria (e.g., InterQual, MCG, eviCore, AIM Specialty Health), validating that all mandatory clinical preconditions are met prior to submission.
  • 3
    Headless RPA Portal Navigation & Packet Dispatch: Automated browser bots authenticate into proprietary payer portals (Availity, Optum, Humana, UnitedHealthcare, CoverMyMeds), populate demographic fields, select correct CPT/HCPCS modifiers, and upload required PDF attachments.
  • 4
    Continuous Status Polling & Payer Feedback Intercept: Bots monitor submission queues on a scheduled hourly cadence, immediately extracting determination notices, approved reference numbers, authorized date ranges, or requests for additional clinical information (RFI).
  • 5
    Closed-Loop PM/EHR Synchronization: Upon approval, the authorization tracking ID and verified CPT units are written back into discrete database fields in your practice management system, clearing the patient on the surgical schedule with zero human data entry.
