STRATEGIC OVERVIEW

Engineered for Seamless Operations & Measurable Outcomes

Modern enterprises confront an explosion of unstructured information—complex PDFs, contracts, emails, and specialized records—that overwhelm manual administrative staff and create operational backlogs.

Nova Arc's AI & Healthcare Technology service domain develops practical, governed artificial intelligence systems and specialized healthcare administration tools. We build autonomous agentic workflows, private generative knowledge assistants, intelligent document processing pipelines, and healthcare administrative integrations.

Our engineering philosophy emphasizes strict data governance, deterministic execution boundaries, and human oversight. In healthcare, our capabilities focus strictly on administrative automation—such as patient registration, scheduling coordination, and document routing—ensuring clear separation from clinical diagnosis and medical decision-making.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical guidance regarding project scope, integrations, and operational requirements.

What services are included under AI & Healthcare Technology? +
The domain unifies Agentic AI, Generative AI Assistants, Intelligent Document Processing (IDP), AI Workflow Automation, Healthcare Administrative Automation, and Healthcare Analytics.
Is customer proprietary data or PHI used to train public AI models? +
No. We implement private, isolated cloud environments with zero data retention for training, ensuring complete data ownership and security.
How does Nova Arc maintain HIPAA compliance in healthcare solutions? +
Our healthcare solutions enforce TLS 1.3 encryption, AES-256 storage, strict administrative separation, ephemeral processing, and full audit logging.
How are errors and low-confidence AI extractions managed? +
Automated confidence scoring routes any document or decision below threshold (e.g. 95%) to visual human review queues before committing data.
Can AI agents integrate with our existing ERP and CRM applications? +
Yes. Agents interact with existing enterprise systems through secure, authenticated REST and GraphQL APIs with strict permission gates.
What is the typical deployment timeline for an AI or healthcare solution? +
Initial pilots for document processing or administrative intake typically deploy in 4 to 8 weeks, with enterprise-wide rollout in 10 to 14 weeks.
