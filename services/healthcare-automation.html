STRATEGIC OVERVIEW

Engineered for Seamless Operations & Measurable Outcomes

Healthcare administrative departments operate under intense pressure: managing complex patient intake documentation, coordinating provider schedules, and navigating intricate administrative payer requirements.

Nova Arc develops Healthcare Technology & Automation solutions engineered exclusively for administrative, operational, and non-clinical workflows. We do not provide clinical decision-making or replace licensed healthcare practitioners.

Our platforms automate digital patient pre-registration, streamline appointment coordination, classify incoming physician referral packets, and support administrative billing data verification, reducing front-end administrative burden and improving clinic efficiency.

PROBLEM & RESOLUTION

Operational Challenges & Technical Resolutions

How Nova Arc overcomes systemic process inefficiencies, operational friction, and technology bottlenecks.

Business Challenge

Business Problem

Front-Desk Registration Bottlenecks

Why It Matters:

Long waiting room queues and illegible paper intake clipboards cause registration delays and data entry errors.

Proposed Resolution: Our digital self-service intake allows patients to complete paperwork on their smartphones prior to their appointment.
Business Challenge

Business Problem

Lost & Delayed Physician Referrals

Why It Matters:

Incoming referral faxes sit unread in digital queues, delaying patient appointments and specialist care.

Proposed Resolution: We deploy automated document classifiers that identify referral orders immediately, extract key details, and alert scheduling coordinators.
Business Challenge

Business Problem

Administrative Claim Formatting Errors

Why It Matters:

Claims rejected by clearinghouses due to missing subscriber IDs or invalid demographic field formatting.

Proposed Resolution: Our administrative validation tools verify EDI formatting and demographic completeness before files are transmitted.

Prerequisites, security safeguards, and human review gates required for dependable execution.

Governance & Controls

Clear Separation from Clinical Practice

Systems are strictly engineered for non-clinical operational, scheduling, and intake processes; they do not evaluate clinical symptoms or provide medical diagnoses.

Governance & Controls

HIPAA & Data Privacy Safeguards

End-to-end data encryption (TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest), Business Associate Agreement (BAA) compliance, and strict access controls ensure protected health information (PHI) remains secure.

Governance & Controls

Human Verification on Extracted Insurance Data

Clinic administrative staff must review extracted member IDs and policy group numbers in a side-by-side verification interface before committing records to the permanent EHR database.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical guidance regarding project scope, integrations, and operational requirements.

How does digital patient intake reduce clinic waiting room times? +
Patients complete registration, medical history disclosures, and insurance card uploads from their own devices prior to arrival, eliminating waiting room clipboards.
How does the system ensure compliance with HIPAA and healthcare privacy laws? +
We enforce end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption, AES-256 at rest, strict administrative separation, ephemeral processing, and tamper-proof access logs.
Can clinical staff verify extracted demographic and insurance data before EHR sync? +
Yes. An administrative queue displays extracted details side-by-side with uploaded insurance card photos, allowing one-click verification before EHR commit.
How are incoming clinical referral documents and faxed records handled? +
Automated document extractors parse incoming digital faxes, extracting patient demographics, referring provider details, and clinical orders into EHR scheduling queues.
Can the platform send automated appointment reminders and intake links? +
Yes. Automated SMS and email sequences send appointment confirmations with personalized, secure registration links 48 and 24 hours prior to visits.
Does this solution replace our existing EHR or Practice Management software? +
No. Nova Arc integrates directly with your existing EHR/PMS (such as Epic, Cerner, Athenahealth, or eClinicalWorks), acting as an administrative acceleration layer.
