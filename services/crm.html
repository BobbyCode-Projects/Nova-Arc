Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

Lead management, sales pipeline tracking, account management, customer onboarding, engagement workflows, customer service processes, and CRM analytics.

Commercial success depends on building lasting relationships with customers across every touchpoint of their lifecycle—from initial lead acquisition to ongoing account management and support.

PROBLEM & RESOLUTION

From Fragmented Workflows to Operational Velocity

Enterprise operations stall when critical customer records, workforce data, and administrative processes remain locked in siloed systems. We engineer unified platforms that automate business workflows and accelerate cross-departmental coordination.

01

Leads Lost in Inbox Chaos

The Challenge

Leads Lost in Inbox Chaos Valuable inbound inquiries sit uncontacted for days because sales teams rely on disorganized email threads and spreadsheets.

Engineered Resolution

We deploy centralized lead queues with instant notification triggers, automated rep assignment, and required response SLAs.

02

Inaccurate Revenue Forecasting

The Challenge

Inaccurate Revenue Forecasting Sales leaders cannot accurately predict quarterly revenue due to unstandardized deal stages and outdated pipeline notes.

Engineered Resolution

Our CRM enforces structured stage completion criteria and automatically calculates weighted pipeline forecasts based on deal velocity.

03

Disconnected Sales & Support Data

The Challenge

Disconnected Sales & Support Data Support agents are unaware of active sales negotiations, while sales reps fail to notice pending customer complaints.

Engineered Resolution

We unify customer interaction histories, support tickets, and sales opportunities in a single comprehensive account overview.

04

Disconnected Customer Communication

The Challenge

Sales, support, and marketing engage clients through fragmented uncoordinated channels. Conflicting customer messages, embarrassing miscommunications, and customer churn.

Engineered Resolution

Centralized omnichannel interaction histories accessible across all client-facing teams.

KEY CAPABILITIES

Core Architecture & Functional Capabilities

Purpose-built engineering modules designed to integrate cleanly into your existing enterprise stack.

Lead Management & Scoring

Automated inbound lead capture, qualification scoring, enrichment, and intelligent distribution to sales reps.

  • Web-to-lead capture forms
  • Configurable qualification scoring
  • Automated territory routing
  • Duplicate detection & merge
  • Predictive lead scoring algorithms based on historical win profiles
  • Automated deduplication and contact data enrichment
  • Territory-based lead routing and sales rep assignment rules
  • Behavioral website interaction tracking and intent signals
  • Inbound lead capture forms with instant CRM sync and notification
  • Lead aging reports and automated recycling of stale prospects

Sales Pipeline & Deal Tracking

Visual Kanban and list views of active opportunities, weighted pipeline forecasts, and milestone tracking.

  • Custom pipeline stages
  • Weighted revenue forecasting
  • Activity timeline tracking
  • Automated follow-up reminders
  • Custom stage gating requiring mandatory deal qualification milestones
  • Weighted revenue forecasting across monthly and quarterly targets
  • Automated contract generation and CPQ (configure, price, quote) integration
  • Deal stagnation alerts when opportunities exceed expected stage duration
  • Competitor battlecard integration directly within opportunity views
  • Win/loss post-mortem tracking and conversion velocity analytics

Customer Onboarding Workflows

Structured client handoff procedures from sales to delivery, document collection, and onboarding checklist tracking.

  • Automated onboarding tasks
  • Document signing integration
  • Client portal access provisioning
  • Milestone progress bars
  • Structured post-sale handoff checklists between sales and client success
  • Automated customer kickoff email sequences and calendar scheduling
  • Implementation milestone tracking with progress percentage bars
  • Customer portal for document submission and onboarding sign-offs
  • Health score calculation based on product adoption and milestone completion
  • Escalation workflows when onboarding projects fall behind scheduled SLAs

Support Desk & Ticketing

Omnichannel customer service ticketing supporting email, web form, and portal requests with SLA countdown timers.

  • Automated ticket assignment
  • SLA escalation timers
  • Knowledge base article linking
  • Customer satisfaction surveys
  • Multi-channel ticket aggregation from email, chat, web, and phone
  • SLA priority matrix with automated escalation timers
  • Internal knowledge base search for rapid support agent resolution
  • Customer satisfaction (CSAT) survey distribution upon ticket closure
  • Canned responses and macro shortcuts for common technical inquiries
  • Product defect escalation pipeline directly to engineering Jira boards
