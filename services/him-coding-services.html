COMPREHENSIVE HIM ARCHITECTURE

The 6 Core Disciplines of Nova Arc HIM & Coding

Every chart undergoes structured ICD-10-CM/PCS and CPT assignment, NCCI edit validation, concurrent clinical review, and non-leading physician engagement to protect hospital and medical group revenue.

Discipline 01 • Inpatient, Outpatient & ProFee

Full-Cycle Medical Coding Services

Our credentialed coding pods—holding CPC (Certified Professional Coder), CCS (Certified Coding Specialist), and CIC (Certified Inpatient Coder) designations—perform high-precision concurrent coding during inpatient stays and retrospective coding post-discharge. We apply exact ICD-10-CM, ICD-10-PCS, CPT, and HCPCS Level II codes with appropriate NCCI modifiers (-25, -59, -X{EPSU}) to maximize first-pass resolution.

  • Guaranteed < 24-Hour SLA: Continuous chart turnaround tracking eliminating Discharged Not Final Billed (DNFB) bottlenecks.
  • Specialty-Specific Pods: Dedicated specialists for Cardiovascular, Orthopedic, Neurosurgery, Emergency, and Interventional Radiology.
  • AI Pre-Scrubbing: Automated algorithmic cross-checking for unbundling, mutually exclusive pairs, and LCD/NCD coverage policies.

Discipline 02 • Case Mix Index Optimization

Clinical Documentation Improvement (CDI) & Chart Auditing

Accurate reimbursement requires clinical notes that fully capture patient acuity. Our ACDIS-certified documentation specialists perform concurrent chart reviews for active inpatients and targeted audits of complex outpatient procedures, ensuring progress notes substantiate proper MS-DRG/APR-DRG assignment.

  • Concurrent Chart Review: Daily pre-discharge reviews identifying missing secondary diagnoses, Comorbidities, and Major Comorbidities (CC/MCC).
  • Acuity Alignment: Ensures Severity of Illness (SOI) and Risk of Mortality (ROM) accurately reflect patient severity on public scorecards.
  • Targeted Provider Coaching: 1-on-1 documentation coaching providing constructive feedback on high-frequency clinical conditions.

Discipline 03 • Value-Based Care & MA

HCC Risk Adjustment & Quality Measure Capture

Medicare Advantage plans, Accountable Care Organizations (ACOs), and clinically integrated networks depend on exact Risk Adjustment Factor (RAF) scores. We systematically audit patient panels for suspect chronic conditions (diabetes with manifestations, chronic kidney disease, CHF) and verify annual re-documentation under CMS-HCC v28 models.

  • Suspect Condition Analytics: Algorithmic screening of historical labs, medications, and clinical notes to flag uncaptured chronic diseases.
  • Point-of-Care EHR Prompts: Pre-encounter physician alerts embedded directly into daily ambulatory clinic schedules.
  • CMS-HCC v28 Mapping: Complete transition support from v24 to v28, managing deleted categories and revised coefficient weights.
