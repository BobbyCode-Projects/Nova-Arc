Discipline 01 • Clean Claim Dispatch

Claims Management & Clearinghouse Scrubbing

Every electronic claim undergoes rigorous multi-tier algorithmic scrubbing prior to transmission. We validate CPT and HCPCS codes against ICD-10 medical necessity criteria, NCCI edits, LCD/NCD guidelines, and payer-specific modifier requirements in real time, achieving 99.4% first-pass clearinghouse acceptance.

  • Autonomous Multi-Tier Scrubbing: Validates HIPAA SNIP levels 1 through 7 before file dispatch to commercial and government clearinghouses.

  • Automated Attachment Routing: Seamless transmission of operative notes, implant invoices (PWK), and secondary EOB documentation.

Denial Management & Root-Cause Appeals

Payer claim rejections demand more than boilerplate resubmissions. Nova Arc combines certified appeal specialists and Registered Nurse (RN) clinical auditors to craft comprehensive appeal packets targeting complex medical necessity (CARC 50), prior authorization (CARC 197), and experimental procedure denials with a 96.8% overturn rate.

  • RN-Led Clinical Appeals: Evidence-based clinical summaries citing peer-reviewed medical literature and Milliman Care Guidelines (MCG).
  • Multi-Level Escalation: Rapid progression through Level 1 reconsiderations, Level 2 external reviews, and state insurance commissioner inquiries.
  • Denial Prevention Analytics: Root causes are systematically tagged by provider, clinic, and CPT code to eradicate recurring claim rejections.

Discipline 07 • Statutory Compliance & Refunds

Credit Balance Resolution & Statutory Refund Compliance

Unresolved credit balances are not just balance sheet liabilities—under the federal Affordable Care Act and CMS 60-Day Rule, failure to report and return overpayments triggers severe False Claims Act exposure. Nova Arc audits credit balances to isolate true overpayments from posting errors, resolving backlogs with audit-ready documentation.

  • Multi-Tier Audit Verification: Differentiates posting errors, duplicate payments, and COB timing issues from genuine overpayments.
  • CMS 60-Day Rule Adherence: Strict compliance workflows preventing extrapolated treble damages and civil monetary penalties.
  • State Escheatment Compliance: Automated tracking of unclaimed patient credit balances conforming to 50-state abandoned property laws.
