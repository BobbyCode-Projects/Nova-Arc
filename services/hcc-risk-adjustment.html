Risk Adjustment / HCC Coding

Capture Clinical Complexity. Strengthen Risk Accuracy.

Connect with us
OVERVIEW

Risk adjustment coding requires accurate identification and documentation of clinically supported conditions that influence patient risk profiles and reimbursement models. Our HCC coding services help healthcare organizations review medical records and identify supported hierarchical condition categories and other risk-relevant diagnoses.

Our specialists evaluate documentation across qualifying encounters to ensure that reported conditions are appropriately supported and coded according to applicable guidelines.

SERVICES PROVIDED

Leverage specialized chart review and coding expertise to capture true clinical complexity.

HCC and risk adjustment coding

Comprehensive assignment of CMS-HCC and HHS-HCC diagnostic categories based strictly on documented clinical evidence and MEAT criteria.
