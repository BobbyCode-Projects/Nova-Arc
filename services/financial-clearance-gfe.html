Regulatory & Consumer Risk

Why Pre-Service Cost Opacity Cripples Healthcare Providers

Price surprise is the leading cause of patient bad debt. Without automated pre-service price transparency, providers face federal fines and uncollected balances.

PROBLEM 01

No Surprises Act Fines & PPDR Arbitration

Failing to provide uninsured or self-pay patients with a compliant Good Faith Estimate (GFE) within 1 to 3 days of scheduling exposes facilities to $10,000 civil monetary penalties per violation. If the final bill exceeds the GFE by just $400, patients can trigger federal dispute arbitrations.

Impact: $10,000 Federal Penalty per Violation • Mandatory PPDR Disputes
PROBLEM 02

High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) Bad Debt

Over 58% of commercially insured workers are enrolled in HDHPs with deductibles averaging $1,750 to $3,200+. When patients receive surprise bills months after surgery, collection probabilities plunge below 25%, turning uncollected patient responsibilities into costly write-offs.

Impact: 75% Drop in Patient Payment Velocity Post-Discharge
PROBLEM 03

Co-Provider Fee Omission & Disjointed Estimates

Under the No Surprises Act, convening facilities must bundle all expected charges—including the surgeon, facility fee, anesthesiologist, pathologist, and surgical implants. Manual staff processes fail to coordinate external co-provider rates, leaving estimates dangerously incomplete.

Impact: Incomplete GFEs • Severe Administrative Non-Compliance
Federal Regulatory Standard

No Surprises Act (NSA) Good Faith Estimate (GFE) Compliance Callout

45 CFR § 149.610 Certified

The federal No Surprises Act (Public Law 116-260) mandates that healthcare facilities issue an itemized Good Faith Estimate (GFE) for all self-pay, uninsured, or commercial out-of-network patients. Nova Arc's automated compliance engine integrates statutory timelines, co-provider rate bundling, and digital delivery verification to ensure 100% audit-proof compliance.

Generated within 1 business day for care scheduled 3–9 days in advance, or within 3 business days for care scheduled 10+ days out.

Unified Co-Provider Bundling

Convening facilities automatically pull in contracted surgeon, anesthesiologist, pathology, and implant fee schedules into a single comprehensive patient document.

$400 PPDR Dispute Threshold

Algorithmic calculations stay within a ± 2.5% tolerance, ensuring final billed amounts never exceed the $400 Patient-Provider Dispute trigger.

Cryptographic Delivery Proof

Frequently Asked Questions

Operational and legal guidance regarding Good Faith Estimates, co-provider coordination, and dispute thresholds.

Under federal regulations, the convening facility (such as an ASC or hospital) is legally responsible for gathering expected charges from all co-providers. Nova Arc maintains pre-configured fee schedules and secure automated request portals for contracted anesthesia, pathology, and surgeon groups. As soon as a case is booked, our engine queries co-provider fee tables automatically, assembling a compliant, bundled Good Faith Estimate without phone calls or manual emails.

If the final billed amount exceeds the GFE by $400 or more, the patient is legally entitled to initiate the federal Patient-Provider Dispute Resolution (PPDR) process within 120 calendar days. If a dispute is filed and the provider loses, the facility is locked into the estimated amount and must pay a non-refundable $25 administrative fee. Nova Arc's algorithmic accuracy keeps final bills well within the $400 threshold, virtually eliminating PPDR dispute exposure.

Nova Arc conducts automated real-time EDI 270/271 balance re-audits 24 hours prior to the date of service. If another healthcare provider has submitted claims that satisfied part of the patient's deductible in the interim, our calculation engine dynamically updates the estimate, adjusts the patient's point-of-service collection amount, and logs the change in the EHR audit trail.
