The Hidden Revenue Bleed

Why Hospital-Based Groups Suffer Catastrophic Underpayments

Unlike outpatient clinics with front-desk copays, hospital-based medical groups (radiologists, anesthesiologists, pathologists, emergency physicians, and hospitalists) bill for complex hospital encounters where automated payer downcoding and misapplied reductions remain invisible to conventional billing software.

Silent CARC 45 "Contractual" Masking

Commercial payers adjudicate claims and bundle arbitrary short-pays under CARC 45 (Charges exceed fee schedule/maximum allowable). Because general billing systems treat CARC 45 as a standard non-collectible write-off, billing teams auto-post the adjustment without checking if the paid amount matched the signed contract exhibit.

Revenue Drain: 3.5% to 6.8% of clean claims written off as "contractual adjustments" in error.

Split-Billing MPPR & Component Conflation

Hospital-based specialists bill the professional component (Modifier 26), while the hospital bills the technical component (TC). Payer adjudication logic routinely misapplies hospital facility Multiple Procedure Payment Reductions (50% reduction) to the physician's professional interpretation, slashing specialist fees unlawfully.

Specialty Impact: $45 to $160 lost on every multi-study imaging encounter or multi-block pathology specimen.

Contractual Complexity & Asymmetry

Commercial agreements feature complex tiered rate cards: percent of Medicare conversion factors, flat fee schedule tables, stop-loss carve-outs, and anesthesia unit multipliers. Without automated mathematical contract modeling, hospital groups negotiate blind and cannot enforce contract compliance at the payment level.

Renegotiation Gap: Groups lose 10%–18% in rate increase leverage during contract renewal cycles.
System Architecture

The 6 Pillars of Contract Modeling & Variance Recovery

How Nova Arc bridges the gap between negotiated managed care contracts and daily payment posting, ensuring every penny owed under contract is recovered.

1

Contract Digitization & Logic Modeling

We ingest master service agreements, fee schedule exhibits (Exhibit A/B), and amendment addenda. Our analysts model exact payment algorithms: % of Medicare RBRVS, commercial flat fee schedules, case rates, anesthesia conversion factors, and stop-loss carve-outs into executable code.

2

Automated Expected-vs-Paid 835 Remittance Audit

Every incoming electronic remittance advice (ERA 835) is matched line-by-line against modeled contract allowables. If Expected Allowable > (Actual Payer Paid + Compliant Patient Responsibility), the system flags the encounter as a negative contractual variance.

3

Specialty Adjudication Rules (Hospital-Based)

Specialty-specific rule modules enforce correct professional component (Mod 26) pricing, audit anesthesia base + exact 15-minute time units + physical status modifiers, and defend surgical pathology specimen block multiplicity (88305) against automated payer downcoding.

4

Automated Contractual Demand Dispute Packages

Identified variances trigger auto-generated formal contractual demand letters citing specific contract article and section numbers, effective fee schedule dates, and ERA payment voucher reference numbers, eliminating hours of manual letter drafting.

5

Joint Operating Committee (JOC) Escalation

Systemic payer adjudicator bugs are aggregated into executive-ready JOC audit dossiers. When a payer repeatedly short-pays an entire specialty code set, our senior managed care liaisons present undeniable mathematical evidence to payer medical directors for bulk retro-adjustments.

6

"What-If" Renegotiation & Parity Modeling

Before renewing or renegotiating commercial agreements, we run "what-if" simulations against trailing 12-month claims data. Groups see the precise financial impact of proposed fee schedule changes down to the individual CPT code, eliminating risky guesswork.

Hospital-based physicians face unique commercial reimbursement challenges: no face-to-face front-desk interaction, split-billing disputes with hospital billing departments, and high clinical volume where small line-item underpayments aggregate into millions in lost revenue.

Radiology & Imaging +$42,000 / Radiologist

Defending Professional Split (Mod 26) Against Facility MPPR

Commercial insurers frequently apply CMS facility technical component Multiple Procedure Payment Reductions (MPPR) to professional interpretation claims (Modifier 26). When radiologists interpret multiple body-area CTs or MRIs, payers illegally slash 50% from second and third professional interpretation fees. Nova Arc's contract model enforces full contracted professional interpretation rates.

  • Audit dual-region CT/MRI professional component allowable rates
  • Reconcile 3D volumetric post-processing interpretations (76376 / 76377)
  • Detect improper bundling of IV contrast professional supervision add-ons
Anesthesia & CRNA Pods +$58,000 / Anesthesiologist

Enforcing Base Units, 15-Minute Time, & Physical Status Modifiers

Anesthesia reimbursement relies on the mathematical formula: (Base Units + Time Units + Qualifying Modifiers) × Conversion Factor. Payers routinely truncate documented anesthesia minutes, disallow qualifying circumstances (99100 extreme age, 99116 hypothermia), or default to low non-participating Medicare conversion factors. Nova Arc cross-examines electronic anesthesia records (AIMS) against fee schedules.

  • Audit exact 15-minute anesthesia time rounding thresholds
  • Enforce Physical Status Modifiers (P3, P4, P5) and emergency add-on units
  • Defend concurrent medical direction concurrency ratios (AA, QK, QX, QZ)
Pathology & Hospitalists +$38,000 / Physician

Multi-Block Specimen Protection & Inpatient Acuity Defense

Surgical pathology groups face aggressive payer edits that bundle multiple tissue specimen blocks (88305 × 4) into a single unit or arbitrarily deny special histochemical stains (88312 / 88313). For hospitalist and emergency groups, payers downcode high-acuity inpatient admissions and ED level 5 visits (99285) without medical record audits. Nova Arc protects documented clinical complexity.

  • Reconcile flow cytometry (88184/88185) and immunohistochemistry stains
  • Eliminate algorithmic downcoding on Level 4/5 ED and inpatient rounding
