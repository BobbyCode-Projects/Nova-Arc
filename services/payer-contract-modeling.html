Underpayment Recovery & Contract Compliance

Identify Payment Gaps. Recover What You Earned.

Connect with us
OVERVIEW

Underpayments can remain hidden when reimbursement is not compared against expected contractual rates and payer terms. Our Underpayment Recovery and Contract Compliance services help healthcare organizations identify potential payment variances and pursue appropriate recovery.

We analyze contractual expectations, payment information, claim details, and applicable reimbursement terms to identify potential underpayments.

SERVICES PROVIDED

Systematic contractual variance auditing, forensic claim research, and underpayment recovery.

Underpayment identification

Systematic cross-comparison of paid remittances against fee schedule contracts to immediately flag line-item payment discrepancies.

Learn more →

Contractual allowance validation

Precision verification of contractual write-offs, verifying that payer adjustment amounts conform strictly to negotiated allowable rates.

Learn more →

Expected reimbursement analysis

Dynamic calculation models simulating expected allowable payments across capitated, per-diem, fee-for-service, and value-based contracts.

Learn more →

Payment variance analysis

Granular analysis isolating variances triggered by incorrect fee schedules, missing modifiers, improper bundling, or improper downcoding.

Learn more →

Payer contract compliance review

Rigorous evaluation assessing whether health plans honor contracted terms, prompt payment regulations, and timely payment clauses.

Learn more →

Underpayment recovery

Structured pursuit and demand package submission targeting health plans to recover documented contractual shortfalls and missing revenue.

Claim-level research

Deep-dive forensic review examining EOB/ERA line items, billing codes, and historical payment patterns to establish undisputed evidence of underpayment.

Learn more →

Payer correspondence

Formal contractual dispute letters, documentation packets, and payer provider relations appeals citing exact agreement sections.

Recovery follow-up

Relentless status tracking and escalation protocols ensuring payers adjudicate and disburse owed underpayment balances promptly.

Underpayment reporting

Executive-level dashboards delivering visibility into payer compliance scores, net recovery totals, variance root causes, and contract yield.
