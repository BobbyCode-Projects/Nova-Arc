Diagnostic Intake Bottlenecks

Why Broken Orders & LCD/NCD Blind Spots Bleed Hospital Revenue

Hospital-based diagnostic groups receive thousands of external physician orders weekly. Missing clinical indications and invalid ABNs cause massive technical claim write-offs.

Unmatched Requisitions & Missing Clinical Indications

Faxed paper orders and disjointed EHR e-referrals frequently arrive with illegible notes, vague diagnostic complaints (e.g. "pain"), or outdated ordering provider NPIs. Radiology technicians and schedulers waste 45+ minutes per case tracking down referring physicians, stalling scanners and frustrating patients.

Retroactive LCD/NCD Denials (CARC Code CO-50)

Medicare MACs publish rigid Local Coverage Determinations (LCDs) specifying which ICD-10 codes establish medical necessity for advanced scans (MRI, CT, PET). Performing an exam under a non-covered diagnosis triggers instant, unappealable CO-50 denials that cannot legally be billed to the patient without an upfront ABN.

Unenforceable ABNs & Statutory Write-Off Hazards

When front-desk staff deliver generic or incomplete Advance Beneficiary Notices (Form CMS-R-131)—failing to provide reasonable cost estimates or specific non-coverage rationale—CMS rules the notice invalid. Providers must write off 100% of both technical and professional components.

Operating Architecture

Referral & Order Matching Workflow

An automated 5-stage ingestion and clinical verification pipeline connecting external referring providers, patient demographic records, and hospital diagnostic scheduling queues.

01

Multi-Channel Order Ingestion & AI Optical Extraction

Instant Ingestion

Orders stream into Nova Arc via HL7 ORM feeds, Direct Secure Messaging, e-referral portals, and inbound digital faxes. Specialized healthcare OCR models extract ordered CPT procedure codes, ICD-10 diagnostic indications, clinical history narratives, patient identifiers, and ordering physician credentials with 99.4% accuracy.

HL7 / FHIR ORM Feed Direct Secure Messaging AI Optical Fax Parser
02

Enterprise MPI Matching & Duplicate MRN Prevention

Demographic Linkage

The requisition details are matched against the hospital's Master Patient Index (MPI). Probabilistic identity algorithms evaluate legal name, date of birth, SSN-4, and address history to bind the requisition to the correct existing medical record or initiate a pristine pre-registration profile.

Zero Overlap Guarantee: Completely eliminates duplicate hospital charts and misattributed diagnostic reports.
03

Ordering Provider NPI & CMS PECOS Registry Validation

Credential Verification

Medicare Part B mandates that referring physicians must be actively enrolled in the CMS Provider Enrollment, Chain, and Ownership System (PECOS). Nova Arc queries the live PECOS national file and state medical licensing databases in sub-second time, confirming the ordering NPI is valid and eligible to order diagnostic imaging.

04

CPT-to-Clinical Indication Cross-Scrubbing & Protocoling

Clinical Matching

The engine compares the specific ordered procedure (e.g. CPT 70553 - MRI Brain with and without contrast) with the patient's diagnostic symptoms (e.g. G44.1 - Vascular headache). If the ordered exam lacks clinical justification, the system alerts the radiology protocoling team or initiates an automated digital query to the referring clinic.

• Contrast vs. Non-Contrast Protocol Check  |  • Prior Authorization Number Verification  |  • Renal Function eGFR Flagging
05

PACS/RIS Scheduling Queue & Closed-Loop Referral Feedback

Closed-Loop Loop

Once cleared and matched, the order is scheduled in the hospital's PACS/RIS (Epic Radiant, Cerner RadNet, GE Centricity) and an automated electronic notification is returned to the referring provider confirming appointment date, scanner location, and clearance status.

Statutory Medicare Compliance

LCD/NCD Medical Necessity Engine & ABN Issuance Logic

Real-time algorithmic evaluation against CMS National and Local Coverage Determinations, paired with automated Form CMS-R-131 Advance Beneficiary Notice delivery.

How the Algorithmic ABN Engine Operates

Under Section 1862(a)(1)(A) of the Social Security Act, Medicare excludes payment for items or services not considered "reasonable and necessary." When an ordered diagnostic exam fails MAC criteria, providers cannot collect from Medicare or the patient unless a valid Advance Beneficiary Notice is signed prior to test execution.

1
Dynamic LCD/NCD Rulebook Sync

Automated weekly updates of all 12 regional Medicare Administrative Contractor (MAC) Local Coverage Determinations (Novitas, Noridian, Palmetto, NGS, First Coast, CGS, WPS) and national NCD policies.

2
Automated Coverage Deficiency Flagging

If ordered ICD-10 diagnosis codes do not appear on the covered list for that specific CPT procedure, the system immediately flags the case as "Medical Necessity Not Established by Medicare."

3
Form CMS-R-131 Automated Population

Populates mandatory statutory fields in real time: specific test name, exact estimated fee (usually $450–$1,200), and custom non-coverage justification explaining why Medicare is unlikely to pay.

4
Downstream Modifier Automation (-GA, -GX, -GZ)

Attaches Modifier -GA to the billing claim if Option 1 is signed (allowing patient balance billing upon denial); attaches Modifier -GZ if an ABN was not secured (prompting mandatory provider write-off without patient harassment).
