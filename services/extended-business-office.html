Operational Crisis Scenarios • When to Deploy EBO

When Do Health Systems & Hospital-Based Groups Deploy EBO Surge Pods?

Even the strongest hospital revenue cycles encounter acute operational disruptions that overwhelm internal billing teams. Nova Arc's Extended Business Office is purpose-built to step in, absorb the shock, and restore balance sheet health.

EHR Go-Live / System Migration Freeze

Migrating to Epic Resolute, Cerner Millennium, or MEDITECH Expanse inevitably triggers a temporary 30-to-60 day cash freeze. Workqueue volumes quadruple, charge routers misfire, and billing clerks struggle with new software. Nova Arc overlays legacy queues, keeping cash flowing while your team masters the new system.

Aged A/R Past 90/120 Days Threatening Timely Filing

When aged receivables exceed 20% of your total ledger, internal staff are forced to triage—working fresh claims while older claims age toward commercial timely-filing limits (typically 90 to 180 days). Nova Arc's surge teams attack aged buckets with algorithmic speed before claims become uncollectible write-offs.

Acute Staffing Shortages & Billing Turnover

Healthcare billing turnover exceeds 25% annually. When senior appeal specialists or inpatient hospital billers resign, institutional knowledge is lost and backlogs instantly mushroom. Nova Arc provides immediate, credentialed surge pods within 5 business days, eliminating recruiting delays and training lag.

M&A Integrations & Acquired Group Ledgers

When a health system acquires community hospitals or hospital-based specialty groups (radiology, anesthesia, pathology), the legacy A/R is often trapped in disparate systems. Nova Arc runs a time-boxed wind-down of legacy practice management software, extracting maximum cash while the enterprise moves forward on Epic or Cerner.
