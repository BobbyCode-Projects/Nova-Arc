STRATEGIC OVERVIEW

Engineered for Seamless Operations & Measurable Outcomes

Corporate knowledge is often scattered across hundreds of legacy PDFs, internal wikis, policy documents, and ticket histories, making it difficult for employees to quickly find accurate information.

Nova Arc engineers private Generative AI Applications and enterprise knowledge assistants. Utilizing secure Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures, our platforms allow employees to query internal corporate data and receive instant, verified answers with clickable citations.

We enforce enterprise security standards: your proprietary data is stored in private vector databases, protected by role-based access permissions, and strictly isolated from public model training datasets.

PROBLEM & RESOLUTION

Operational Challenges & Technical Resolutions

How Nova Arc overcomes systemic process inefficiencies, operational friction, and technology bottlenecks.

Business Challenge

Business Problem

Hallucinations & False Assertions

Why It Matters:

Public chatbots generate convincing but entirely fabricated information that misleads staff and damages credibility.

Proposed Resolution: Our RAG architecture constrains the model to synthesize answers solely from retrieved corporate documents, rejecting unsupported queries.
Business Challenge

Business Problem

Confidential Intellectual Property Leakage

Why It Matters:

Employees paste sensitive internal financial or customer data into public AI tools, risking commercial espionage.

Proposed Resolution: We deploy private enterprise assistants within isolated cloud environments where inputs are encrypted and never used for model training.
Business Challenge

Business Problem

Unauthorized Access to Sensitive Data

Why It Matters:

All employees gaining access to executive compensation, pending M&A plans, or confidential HR records.

Proposed Resolution: Our knowledge search enforces document-level permissions, ensuring assistants answer queries using only files the specific employee is authorized to see.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical guidance regarding project scope, integrations, and operational requirements.

How does a private enterprise assistant ensure factual accuracy without hallucinating? +
Responses are grounded strictly in retrieved chunks from your verified internal documents, providing verifiable source links for every statement.
Can different departments access only their authorized knowledge sources? +
Yes. Access control lists (ACLs) mirror your corporate permissions, ensuring employees query only the documents their role is authorized to view.
What document formats can be ingested into the enterprise knowledge base? +
We ingest PDFs, Microsoft Word/Excel/PowerPoint documents, text files, HTML wikis, and structured databases automatically.
How are updates to existing company policies and procedures synchronized? +
Automated incremental scrapers detect document changes in cloud drives or wikis, re-indexing vector embeddings within minutes of publication.
Is proprietary company IP protected from leaking into external model training? +
Yes. We use zero-data-retention enterprise agreements with isolated tenant containers, ensuring company data is never used to train public models.
Can the assistant summarize long technical or legal documents on demand? +
Yes. Users can upload multi-page contracts, manuals, or financial filings, receiving instant executive summaries, key terms, and risk factor callouts.
