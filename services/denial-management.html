The Revenue Leak

Why Health Systems Lose Millions to Algorithmic Denials

Commercial health plans and Medicare Advantage insurers have automated claim rejections using aggressive clinical AI filters. Traditional hospital business offices struggle to keep pace with mounting appeal deadlines and evidentiary burdens.

EXPOSURE 01

Algorithmic Inpatient Downgrades & Clinical Denials

Payers deploy proprietary claim adjudication algorithms that automatically downgrade acute inpatient admissions (MS-DRG) to outpatient observation status (CARC 50 / 57), or reject high-acuity secondary diagnoses (Sepsis, Acute Kidney Injury, Encephalopathy) without human clinical review, slashing reimbursement by $12,000 to $35,000 per stay.

Financial Risk: 65% of Commercial Hospital DRG Denials Go Unappealed
EXPOSURE 02

Manual Assembly Fatigue & Timely Filing Forfeiture

Drafting clinical appeal packets requires pulling hundreds of pages: operative notes, vitals flowsheets, physician orders, and medical policy bulletins. Strained hospital CBO staff cannot keep up, resulting in generic "form letters" that are instantly rejected, or missing the insurer's strict 30 to 60-day timely filing window entirely.

Financial Risk: Permanent Timely Filing Write-Offs (CARC 29)
EXPOSURE 03

Repetitive Denial Loops & Zero Root-Cause Feedback

Hospitals treat denials reactively, re-working the same technical defects month after month—missing pre-authorizations (CARC 197), unbundled CPT codes (CARC 97), or invalid ordering NPIs (CARC 183)—without feeding root causes back to scheduling, patient access, or clinical documentation teams to prevent recurrence.

Financial Risk: Perpetual Cost-to-Collect Inflation & Chronic Margin Erosion

Executive FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key insights into CARC/RARC triage, clinical appeal letter construction, Medicare Advantage parity, and closed-loop denial prevention.

When an electronic remittance advice (EDI 835) arrives, Nova Arc’s intake engine scans every Claim Adjustment Segment (CAS). The system extracts the Claim Adjustment Group Code (such as CO for Contractual Obligation or PR for Patient Responsibility), the primary Claim Adjustment Reason Code (CARC), and Remittance Advice Remark Codes (RARC). Denials are mapped into five core workqueues: (1) Medical Necessity & Level of Care; (2) Prior Authorization & Pre-Certification; (3) Coding & NCCI Unbundling; (4) Coordination of Benefits & Eligibility; and (5) Timely Filing & Gateway Rejections. Workqueues are prioritized algorithmically by recoverable dollar value, payer timely filing deadlines, and historical overturn probability.

Commercial payers routinely use optical scanning algorithms to instantly reject boilerplate "form" appeal letters. Nova Arc creates fully custom, patient-specific appeal briefs authored and audited by Registered Nurse (RN) clinical appeal specialists. Each letter incorporates: (1) An exact clinical timeline referencing objective vital signs, nursing notes, and diagnostic test timestamps; (2) Direct citations of recognized clinical benchmarks (such as Milliman Care Guidelines, InterQual, or specialty clinical consensus criteria); (3) Cross-references to the payer’s specific published Clinical Policy Bulletin; and (4) Legal citations under federal statutes (such as ERISA §503 for commercial plans and 42 CFR §422 for Medicare Advantage).

Under CMS Final Rule 4201-F (effective January 2024), Medicare Advantage Organizations (MAOs) are federally required to provide coverage for basic benefits that is at least equivalent to Traditional Medicare National Coverage Determinations (NCDs) and Local Coverage Determinations (LCDs). MAOs cannot use internal proprietary criteria to restrict coverage when an NCD or LCD exists. For short-stay inpatient admissions, Nova Arc strictly enforces compliance with the CMS Two-Midnight Rule (42 CFR §412.3). If an MA plan wrongfully upholds an inpatient denial, we escalate directly to the CMS Independent Review Entity (IRE), where provider overturn rates historically exceed 75%.

Nova Arc operates under a strict contractual Sub-48-Hour Appeal Submission SLA. As soon as an 835 denial remit posts, our automated pipeline harvests the corresponding clinical chart notes, lab reports, and billing data from the hospital EHR. High-priority clinical appeals are assigned to dedicated RN auditor pods within 4 operating hours, drafted, peer-reviewed, and transmitted electronically via EDI 275 Additional Information attachments or secure payer clearinghouse portals well in advance of insurer timely filing deadlines.

Nova Arc closes the loop between back-end dispute resolution and front-end revenue cycle operations. Every overturned and upheld denial is tagged with root-cause metadata (e.g., ordering clinic, attending physician, CPT code combination, or payer policy version). Granular reports are fed back to: (1) Patient Access: Updating front-end eligibility and authorization workqueues; (2) Clinical Documentation Improvement (CDI): Coaching physicians on documenting specific clinical acuity indicators; and (3) Clearinghouse Scrubbers: Creating custom payer edit rules that intercept flawed claims before initial submission.
