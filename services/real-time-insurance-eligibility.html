Front-End Root Causes

Why Traditional Front-Desk Verification Fails

Over 70% of downstream denials originate at intake. Here is why conventional payer portals and manual batch checks cost medical groups millions in avoidable write-offs.

PROBLEM 01

Overnight Batch Lag & Manual Portal Lookups

Practices relying on overnight clearinghouse batches miss day-of plan cancellations and mid-month coverage terminations. Staff waste 8 to 15 minutes per patient logging into fragmented payer portals, resulting in lobby check-in bottlenecks and missed copays.

Impact: CARC 27 (Expenses Incurred After Coverage Terminated)
PROBLEM 02

Undetected Coordination-of-Benefits (COB) Conflicts

Front-desk intake staff cannot manually decipher intricate commercial birthday rules, Medicare Secondary Payer (MSP) questionnaires, or workers' compensation primacy. Misordered coverage leads to immediate denials that take months of rework.

Impact: CARC 22 (Payment Adjusted: COB Primacy Disputed)
PROBLEM 03

Opaque Procedure-Level Benefit Exclusions

A basic "active coverage" green checkmark does not confirm whether a specialist visit, in-office injection, ultrasound, or minor surgery is covered. High-deductible plan details remain concealed, forcing practices to chase uncollected balances after claim adjudication.

Impact: Bad Debt & Disputed Patient Out-of-Pocket Balances

Coordination-of-Benefits (COB) Conflict Detection

How Nova Arc automatically identifies and resolves primary vs. secondary insurance conflicts prior to encounter check-in, preventing fatal CARC 22 denials.

Conventional Intake (Failure Mode) CARC 22 Denial

Dual Coverage Scenario: NAIC Birthday Rule Conflict

A pediatric patient arrives with coverage under both parents. The parent hands the receptionist their Aetna Commercial PPO card first. The receptionist inputs Aetna as Primary without cross-checking the other parent's birth date.

Downstream Consequence: Aetna pays first, but 60 days later performs a post-payment recoupment, citing the father's BCBS Horizon plan as primary under the Birthday Rule (Father DOB: March 14; Mother DOB: August 22). Claim is denied, balance aging past 90 days.
Average Resolution Cost: $48 per disputed claim in billing staff rework
