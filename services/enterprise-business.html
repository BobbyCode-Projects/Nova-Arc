STRATEGIC OVERVIEW

Engineered for Seamless Operations & Measurable Outcomes

Operational friction in growing enterprises typically stems from disconnected business applications, manual handoffs between departments, and fragmented customer and employee data.

Nova Arc's Enterprise Business Solutions domain designs, builds, and integrates modern business software systems. We deliver bespoke CRM platforms, robust HRMS platforms, policy-driven business process automation (BPA), and custom enterprise portals.

By connecting departmental databases, automating repetitive administrative tasks, and establishing unified data visibility, we help organizations break down silos, enhance workforce productivity, and build a scalable foundation for business growth.

KEY CAPABILITIES

Core Architecture & Functional Capabilities

Purpose-built engineering modules designed to integrate cleanly into your existing enterprise stack.

Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

Omnichannel lead management, sales pipeline tracking, customer support ticketing, and relationship intelligence.

  • 360-degree customer profile consolidation across omni-channel touchpoints
  • Intelligent lead scoring, automated routing, and sales territory management
  • Configurable B2B sales pipeline stages with opportunity stage probability forecasting
  • CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote) automation with multi-tier approval hierarchies
  • Omnichannel customer support ticketing with SLA countdowns and escalation protocols
  • Field service management with mobile job assignment, parts tracking, and work order signoffs
  • Automated email campaign orchestration and interaction history synchronization
  • Contract renewal tracking and automated customer churn risk detection
  • Deep CRM integration with ERP backends (Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics, Zoho)
  • Real-time executive sales performance dashboards and revenue conversion reporting

Human Resource Management (HRMS)

Complete workforce lifecycle administration, onboarding, attendance, payroll automation, and performance reviews.

  • Complete employee lifecycle management from recruitment through offboarding
  • Automated applicant tracking system (ATS) with resume parsing and interview scheduling
  • Digital onboarding checklists, e-document signing, and asset allocation tracking
  • Biometric and geofenced attendance tracking with automated overtime calculation
  • Multi-country payroll computation with statutory tax deductions and direct bank transfers
  • Flexible leave policy configuration with automated approval matrices and balance ledgers
  • Performance management systems with OKR tracking, 360-degree feedback, and appraisal cycles
  • Employee self-service (ESS) portals for payslips, tax declarations, and expense claims
  • Learning Management System (LMS) integration with compliance training certifications
  • HR analytics dashboards for workforce attrition, headcount forecasting, and diversity metrics

Business Process Automation (BPA)

Automated approval hierarchies, robotic task execution, document workflows, and operational bottleneck reduction.

  • Visual drag-and-drop workflow builders for cross-departmental business processes
  • Robotic Process Automation (RPA) bots for high-volume repetitive administrative tasks
  • Document approval matrices with conditional branching and automated reminder escalation
  • Automated financial reconciliation between payment gateways, banks, and general ledgers
  • Customer credit verification and risk scoring automation for loan and trade accounts
  • Employee expense report auditing with OCR receipt scanning and policy validation
  • Automated contract compliance auditing and regulatory deadline tracking
  • Cross-system data synchronization eliminating duplicate manual data entry
  • Event-driven webhook triggers and scheduled batch job execution pipelines
  • Comprehensive audit logging and compliance reporting for ISO and SOC 2 audits

Enterprise Application Development

Custom cloud-native web and mobile applications engineered to replace rigid legacy software.

  • Custom cloud-native microservices architecture designed for enterprise scalability
  • Responsive web applications built with modern frontend frameworks (React, Angular, Next.js)
  • Native and cross-platform enterprise mobile apps (iOS, Android, Flutter, React Native)
  • Secure role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA), and Single Sign-On (SSO)
  • High-performance relational and NoSQL database modeling (PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis)
  • Automated CI/CD pipelines with automated unit, integration, and security vulnerability testing
  • Containerized application orchestration via Kubernetes, Docker, and AWS ECS
  • High-availability clustering with auto-scaling, load balancing, and multi-region failover
  • Legacy system modernization, code refactoring, and database schema migration
  • Comprehensive API documentation via OpenAPI/Swagger and automated developer portals

System Integration & Data Connectivity

Secure API gateways, ERP/CRM bi-directional data synchronization, and enterprise middleware pipelines.

  • Enterprise Service Bus (ESB) and modern event-driven API gateway architectures
  • Pre-built connectors for leading enterprise ERPs (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Dynamics 365)
  • Bi-directional ETL and ELT data pipelines for cloud data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift)
  • Real-time pub/sub messaging architectures using Apache Kafka and RabbitMQ
  • Secure EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) processing for X12, EDIFACT, and AS2 standards
  • RESTful and GraphQL API design with rate limiting, throttling, and OAuth2 security
  • Master Data Management (MDM) with golden record deduplication and entity resolution
  • Legacy mainframe and on-premise database bridge connectors with low-latency polling
  • Data transformation engines converting XML, JSON, CSV, and proprietary binary payloads
  • Real-time integration health monitoring, payload dead-letter queues, and automated retry mechanisms

What services comprise Enterprise Business Solutions? +
