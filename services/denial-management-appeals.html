Denial Management & Appeals

Turn Denials into Opportunities for Recovery.

Connect with us
OVERVIEW

Denials can significantly affect reimbursement, cash flow, and revenue cycle performance. Our Denial Management and Appeals services help healthcare organizations identify denial causes, prioritize accounts, perform root-cause analysis, and pursue appropriate resolution.

We support the complete denial lifecycle from identification and categorization through appeal preparation, submission, follow-up, and reporting.

SERVICES PROVIDED

Comprehensive root-cause analysis, clinical review, and structured appeal recovery.

Denial identification and categorization

Rapid ingestion and classification of ERA 835 adjustment codes into clear operational buckets, including clinical, technical, administrative, and eligibility denials.

Learn more →

Denial root-cause analysis

Comprehensive diagnostic tracing to pinpoint underlying breakdown points across front-end registration, clinical documentation, coding, or billing edits.

Learn more →

Clinical and technical denial review

Specialized clinical evaluation by certified auditors and technical billing checks to determine viability and appeal strategy.

Learn more →

Appeal preparation

Drafting compelling, customized appeal letters backed by relevant medical records, operative notes, clinical practice guidelines, and payer policy citations.

Learn more →

Appeal submission

Multi-channel submission via direct payer portals, clearinghouses, certified mail, or secure electronic attachments within mandated appeal timeframes.

Learn more →

Payer follow-up

Persistent, structured status inquiries tracking open appeals through first-level reviews, reconsiderations, peer-to-peer discussions, and administrative hearings.

Learn more →

Authorization denial management

Forensic review and retro-authorization appeals addressing missing, expired, or truncated prior authorizations and procedural mismatch issues.

Learn more →

Medical necessity denial review

Evidence-based clinical defense defending inpatient admissions, high-cost imaging, and surgical procedures using Milliman and InterQual guidelines.

Learn more →

Timely filing denial analysis

Detailed validation of electronic EDI submission timestamps, clearinghouse 277 acceptance logs, and payer acknowledgment receipts to overturn untimely filing rejections.

Learn more →

Denial trend reporting

Executive reporting and actionable dashboards highlighting payer-specific denial rates, overturn success rates, recovery net dollars, and prevention opportunities.

Learn more →
BENEFITS

Increase Denial Recovery

Structured follow-up and appeals help organizations recover appropriately payable claims.

Identify Root Causes

Trend analysis helps identify recurring process issues contributing to denials.

Reduce Preventable Denials

Insights from denial analysis can support corrective actions at the front end.

Improve Revenue Performance

Effective denial management helps protect earned reimbursement and reduce revenue leakage.
