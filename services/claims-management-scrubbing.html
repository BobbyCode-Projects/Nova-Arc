Claims Submission & Scrubbing — EDI 837

Clean Claims In. Faster Processing. Fewer Preventable Errors.

Connect with us
OVERVIEW

Accurate claim submission is critical to timely reimbursement. Our Claims Submission and Scrubbing services help healthcare organizations validate claims before transmission and identify common demographic, coding, billing, and payer-related errors.

We support electronic claim workflows using appropriate EDI transactions, including the 837 healthcare claim transaction, helping improve claim quality before submission.

SERVICES PROVIDED

Intelligent pre-submission scrubbing and seamless EDI 837 claim transmission.

Claim preparation and submission

Systematic compiling, formatting, and electronic dispatch of professional (837P) and institutional (837I) healthcare claims to commercial and government payers.

Learn more →

EDI 837 transaction support

Comprehensive compliance with ANSI X12 837 standards, ensuring syntax validation, proper loop and segment formatting, and secure clearinghouse transmission.

Learn more →

Claim scrubbing

Multi-tiered algorithmic validation checking diagnosis-procedure crosswalks, gender and age consistency, medical necessity, and CCI bundling rules.

Learn more →

Demographic validation

Verification of patient demographics, subscriber details, policy numbers, and guarantor information to prevent avoidable registration discrepancies.

Learn more →

Insurance information validation

Verification of active coverage, effective dates, primary/secondary coordination of benefits (COB), and payer ID routing before submission.

Learn more →

Coding and billing edits

Pre-submission audit rules verifying appropriate CPT/HCPCS codes, ICD-10 specificity, required billing modifiers, and accurate units of service.

Learn more →

Payer-specific validation

Custom rules engine applying distinct commercial, Medicare, Medicaid, and regional payer billing requirements and local coverage determinations (LCDs).

Learn more →

Duplicate claim identification

Automated pre-transmission safeguards identifying identical or overlapping service dates, provider IDs, and procedures to prevent duplicate denials.

Learn more →

Claim rejection analysis

Rapid parsing of 277CA clearinghouse rejection notices and payer acknowledgment reports to quickly remediate errors before claims age.

Learn more →

Pre-submission quality checks

Final automated and expert quality-assurance reviews confirming complete documentation attachments, authorization numbers, and clean claim viability.

Learn more →
BENEFITS
