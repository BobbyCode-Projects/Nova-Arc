Gateway Friction & Revenue Traps

The High Cost of Front-End Gateway Friction

Traditional clearinghouse scrubbers rely on rudimentary syntax checks that miss complex clinical bundling and volatile commercial payer policies, leaving millions in limbo.

EXPOSURE 01

Clearinghouse Latency & Unworked 277CA Rejections

Electronic claim rejections (EDI 277CA) linger in clearinghouse portals for an average of 5.8 days before billing staff manually triage them. Because rejected claims never enter the payer's adjudication pipeline, timely filing windows expire while Discharged Not Final Billed (DNFB) balances balloon.

Financial Threat: $380,000+ Trapped in Unbilled Gateway Queues
EXPOSURE 02

Payer Policy Volatility & NCCI Bundling Conflicts

CMS quarterly NCCI updates, Medically Unlikely Edits (MUEs), and proprietary commercial bulletin changes (UHC, BCBS, Aetna, Cigna) cause endless technical denials. Generic scrubbers fail to account for carrier-specific modifier rules (-50 vs -RT/-LT, -X{EPSU}, -SG), stripping surgical margins.

Financial Threat: 8% to 12% Preventable Revenue Leakage on Surgeries
EXPOSURE 03

Disjointed Secondary Crossover & COB Failures

When primary remit adjustments (CAS segments with CO, PR, OA groups) fail to translate cleanly into secondary 837 claims, secondary submissions stall. Without automated crossover engines, claims age past 90 days, turning collectible balances into administrative bad debt.

Financial Threat: Bloated Aged A/R > 90 Days & Expired Timely Filing

Executive FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key operational questions regarding clearinghouse rejections, payer-specific edits, Autonomous Scrubber AI, and ASC billing.

A clearinghouse rejection (communicated via EDI 277CA or 999 Functional Acknowledgement) happens at the gateway level before the claim enters the payer's adjudication database. It is triggered by syntax errors, invalid member IDs, or unacknowledged NPIs. Because the claim was never formally received by the payer, the timely filing clock continues to run unchecked. A payer denial (communicated via EDI 835 remittance with CARC and RARC codes) occurs after the payer adjudicates the claim. Nova Arc triages 277CA rejections within 4 operating hours, eliminating dormant gateway dropouts and preventing unnecessary administrative write-offs.

Our Claims Management service is powered natively by the Nova Arc Autonomous Claims Scrubber AI Platform. The AI engine performs sub-second pre-bill predictive scoring against 15,000+ national and regional payer rules, dynamically suggesting or appending appropriate clinical modifiers (-25, -59, -XU), validating LCD/NCD diagnosis linkages, and flagging missing prior authorization numbers. Certified medical billing specialists oversee the AI queue, reviewing complex edge cases and maintaining 100% audit defensibility.

Ambulatory Surgery Centers operate under complex multi-procedure reduction cascades (e.g. 100% allowance for the highest-weighted surgical code, 50% for secondary codes). Nova Arc automatically sequences surgical procedures by contract allowance to protect maximum yield. For high-cost implants (such as anchors, screws, or mesh under HCPCS C1713 or L8699), our engine automatically suppresses packaged implant codes on Medicare claims while appending required manufacturer invoice attachments via electronic PWK01/PWK02 segments on commercial claims to ensure full pass-through payment.

Our rules engine is maintained across three continuous tiers: (1) Federal CMS NCCI & MUE Updates are synchronized quarterly upon official publication; (2) Medicare MAC Local and National Coverage Determinations (LCD/NCD) are refreshed weekly; and (3) Commercial Carrier Technical Bulletins (UHC, Anthem/BCBS, Aetna, Cigna, Humana) are reviewed and incorporated in real time as medical payment policies evolve.
