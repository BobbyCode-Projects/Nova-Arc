EDI Enrollment Services

Connect Seamlessly. Transact Reliably. Operate Efficiently.

Connect with us
OVERVIEW

Electronic Data Interchange enables healthcare organizations to exchange claims, remittance, eligibility, and other transactions electronically with payers and clearinghouses. Our EDI Enrollment Services help organizations establish and maintain the required electronic transaction relationships.

We support enrollment coordination, payer requirements, transaction setup, status tracking, and issue resolution.

SERVICES PROVIDED

Comprehensive EDI enrollment, clearinghouse coordination, and connectivity management.

EDI enrollment coordination

Centralized coordination across providers, practice locations, and hospital facilities to establish smooth, end-to-end electronic transaction pipelines.

Payer enrollment support

Comprehensive assistance navigating complex commercial, Medicare MAC, Medicaid, and managed care enrollment criteria and documentation requirements.

Learn more →

Clearinghouse enrollment

Seamless setup and configuration across leading clearinghouse gateways, ensuring proper SFTP endpoints, submitter IDs, and trading partner links.

Learn more →

ERA/835 enrollment

Setup and validation of Electronic Remittance Advice (ERA/835) feeds to automate posting workflows, eliminate paper checks, and accelerate bank reconciliation.

Claims/837 enrollment

Configuration and testing of 837P Professional and 837I Institutional electronic claim submission channels for uninterrupted, clean transmission.

Eligibility transaction enrollment

Enabling real-time 270/271 electronic eligibility and benefit verification channels to streamline upfront patient verification and reduce claim denials.

Enrollment status tracking

Continuous milestone tracking and transparent reporting across all submitted enrollment packets, eliminating blind spots and stagnant requests.

Payer communication

Proactive liaison with payer EDI departments and clearinghouse technical contacts to accelerate approvals, verify credentials, and resolve delays.

Enrollment issue resolution

Rapid troubleshooting and resolution of enrollment rejections, missing tax forms, credential mismatches, and clearinghouse routing discrepancies.

Transaction connectivity support
