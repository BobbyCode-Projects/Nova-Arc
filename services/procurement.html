DIGITAL COMMERCE & SUPPLY CHAIN

Procurement & Vendor Management

Digital purchase requisitions, approval workflows, supplier onboarding, vendor portals, purchase order management, invoice workflows, and spend visibility.

Corporate procurement processes are frequently hindered by decentralized purchasing, cumbersome manual approvals, missing contract terms, and time-consuming invoice reconciliations that tie up accounts payable teams. Nova Arc develops centralized Procurement & Vendor Management platforms that enforce spending policies, streamline purchase requisition approvals, and provide secure self-service portals for external suppliers.

Our platforms automate the entire procure-to-pay (P2P) cycle. By digitizing supplier onboarding, tracking vendor compliance, and automating three-way matching, we help organizations eliminate maverick spend, capture early payment discounts, and gain complete control over corporate expenditures.

PROBLEM & RESOLUTION

Operational Challenges & Technical Resolutions

How Nova Arc overcomes systemic process inefficiencies, operational friction, and technology bottlenecks.

Business Challenge

Business Problem

Uncontrolled Maverick Spending

Why It Matters:

Employees buy supplies outside contracted vendor catalogs, losing volume discounts and blowing project budgets.

Proposed Resolution: We enforce strict requisition controls where non-catalog purchases require explicit executive justification and approval.
Business Challenge

Business Problem

Slow Purchase Order Approvals

Why It Matters:

Critical purchasing requests get buried in manager inboxes, delaying project timelines and operational procurement.

Proposed Resolution: Our mobile-enabled approval workflows allow managers to approve requests in one click, auto-escalating overdue approvals.
Business Challenge

Business Problem

Accounts Payable Reconciliation Burden

Why It Matters:

AP accountants spend hundreds of hours manually comparing paper invoices against POs to identify billing overcharges.

Proposed Resolution: We automate three-way matching, auto-clearing compliant invoices within pre-set price and quantity tolerance thresholds.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical guidance regarding project scope, integrations, and operational requirements.

How does automated three-way matching prevent invoice fraud and overpayment? +
The system automatically verifies purchase order line items, receiving dock delivery slips, and vendor invoices before releasing payment.
Can vendors submit digital invoices and update tax information directly? +
Yes. The vendor portal allows suppliers to upload invoices, verify payment remittance status, and update GST/tax documents securely.
How does the platform enforce corporate purchasing approval thresholds? +
Configurable approval matrices route requisitions based on department, cost center, and expenditure amount with mobile one-tap approval.
Does the procurement portal support RFP and vendor bidding workflows? +
Yes. Procurement officers can publish requests for quotes (RFQs), collect structured supplier bids, and compare proposals side-by-side.
How are vendor compliance and certifications tracked? +
The system tracks supplier insurance certificates, ISO credentials, and tax compliance, flagging expiring documents before PO release.
Can the system capture early payment discounts from suppliers? +
Yes. Invoices with dynamic discounting terms (e.g., 2/10 net 30) are prioritized in payment schedules to optimize working capital.
