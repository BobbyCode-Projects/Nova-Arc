Patient Billing & Self-Pay Collections

Simplify Patient Billing. Strengthen Patient Financial Outcomes.

Connect with us
OVERVIEW

Patient financial responsibility is an important component of the healthcare revenue cycle. Our Patient Billing and Self-Pay Collection services help organizations manage patient balances while maintaining clear, respectful, and patient-focused financial communication.

We support billing workflows, balance inquiries, payment collection activities, and appropriate account follow-up.

SERVICES PROVIDED

Transparent billing communications, flexible payment options, and patient-centric resolution.

Patient statement support

Generating plain-language, easy-to-read electronic and paper statements clarifying insurance payments, adjustments, and remaining patient responsibility.

Learn more →

Self-pay account management

Structured workflows managing uninsured encounters, catastrophic high-deductible plans, and self-pay service discount schedules.

Learn more →

Patient balance follow-up

Compassionate, timely engagement across secure SMS text-to-pay, email reminders, and phone communication to address open balances before they age.

Learn more →

Payment collection support

Seamless omnichannel payment processing enabling patients to settle balances via online portals, mobile payment links, phone, and card-on-file.

Learn more →

Billing inquiry assistance

Dedicated, courteous patient financial service representatives answering billing questions, explaining EOB breakdowns, and resolving concerns.

Learn more →

Financial responsibility communication

Proactive outreach educating patients on deductible thresholds, copay obligations, and transparent cost estimates prior to service.

Learn more →

Payment arrangement support

Establishing customized, structured installment payment plans and hardship assistance tailored to individual patient financial circumstances.

Learn more →

Account documentation

Detailed logging of all patient interactions, payment plan commitments, address updates, and dispute resolutions within practice management systems.

Learn more →

Balance research

Forensic investigation into disputed charges, missing insurance secondary payments, or unapplied patient credits prior to statements.

Learn more →

Self-pay reporting

Comprehensive analytics monitoring collection rates, payment arrangement compliance, days in self-pay A/R, and patient satisfaction scores.
