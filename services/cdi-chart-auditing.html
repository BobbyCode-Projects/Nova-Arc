Clinical Documentation Improvement (CDI)

Better Documentation. Clearer Clinical Story. Stronger Revenue Integrity.

Connect with us
OVERVIEW

Clinical documentation serves as the foundation for accurate coding, quality reporting, risk adjustment, and appropriate reimbursement. Our CDI services help healthcare organizations identify opportunities to improve the completeness, clarity, specificity, and clinical consistency of provider documentation.

Our teams collaborate with clinical and coding stakeholders to identify documentation opportunities while maintaining the integrity of the medical record.

SERVICES PROVIDED

Leverage clinical documentation specialists to capture the full clinical story with integrity.

Clinical documentation review

Comprehensive concurrent and retrospective clinical chart review analyzing provider notes, diagnostic findings, and treatment plans.

Documentation gap identification

Systematic detection of missing clinical details, ambiguous entries, conflicting statements, and unaddressed diagnostic findings.

CDI opportunity identification

Identification of legitimate documentation enhancements reflecting true patient acuity, disease severity, and complexity of care.

Provider clarification support

Structured, non-leading physician query workflows to elicit documentation clarity without influencing clinical decision-making.

Clinical indicator review

Clinical analysis connecting abnormal lab results, vital signs, medication orders, and nursing assessments with documented diagnoses.

Diagnosis specificity review

Documentation consistency checks

Concurrent and retrospective review

CDI quality monitoring

Provider education support
