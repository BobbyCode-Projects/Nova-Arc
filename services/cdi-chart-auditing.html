Clinical Acuity Vulnerabilities

Why Documentation Gaps & Unchecked CAC Systems Threaten Health Systems

Vague clinical progress notes, uncaptured secondary comorbidities, and unmonitored Computer-Assisted Coding (CAC) algorithms silently drain millions in legitimate reimbursement and inflate mortality metrics.

Acuity Undercoding & Missed CC/MCC Capture

Physicians treat high-acuity illnesses but often record non-specific diagnoses (e.g., "altered mental status" instead of metabolic encephalopathy, or "acute renal failure" without staging). Missing even a single Major Complication or Comorbidity (MCC) routinely shifts an inpatient encounter down an entire MS-DRG tier, forfeiting $4,500 to $9,000 per admission.

Impact: Case Mix Index (CMI) erosion and distorted hospital quality scorecards.

CAC Algorithm Blind Spots & Phantom Revenue

Enterprise Natural Language Processing (NLP) tools in Computer-Assisted Coding (CAC) engines frequently misread negated clinical terms, grab historical conditions as acute, or miss underlying etiologies. Relying on raw CAC suggestions without rigorous human coder auditing exposes health systems to severe RAC recoupments, false claim scrutiny, and unbundling penalties.

Impact: Up to 26% discrepancy rate between unverified CAC suggestions and audited charts.

Physician Query Fatigue & Delayed DNFB

Complex, verbose, or improperly formulated queries frustrate physicians, causing response rates to plummet below 60%. When queries stall, accounts linger in Discharged Not Final Billed (DNFB) workqueues for weeks, inflating days in A/R and strangling hospital operational liquidity.

Impact: Chart latency exceeding 9+ days and widespread clinical physician frustration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clinical Documentation & Chart Auditing FAQ

Answers to common questions from Chief Medical Officers, HIM Directors, and Chief Financial Officers regarding Nova Arc CDI services.

Nova Arc specialists work directly inside your existing Computer-Assisted Coding (CAC) and EHR infrastructure via secure, encrypted VPN or virtual desktop (Citrix/VMware). Whether your health system operates 3M 360 Encompass, Optum CAC, or Nuance Clintegrity, our team reviews CAC-suggested codes in your native workqueues, validates natural language processing output against clinical notes, and assigns approved codes without exporting data or introducing third-party platform friction.
We strictly enforce the AHIMA / ACDIS Guidelines for Achieving a Compliant Query Practice. Every query template presents clinically supported multiple-choice options (including "Clinically Undetermined", "Other", and "Not Clinically Significant") without nudging the clinician toward a specific reimbursement outcome. All queries cite objective clinical indicators (e.g., lab values, vitals, diagnostic imaging, and therapeutic interventions) already present in the medical record.
Concurrent CDI occurs while the patient is actively hospitalized in an inpatient bed. The specialist reviews daily progress notes and interacts with the care team to clarify clinical documentation prior to discharge. Retrospective Chart Auditing occurs post-discharge during the coding window (pre-bill) or after claim adjudication to evaluate coder accuracy, verify MS-DRG/APR-DRG assignment, audit unverified CAC suggestions, and identify systemic documentation education opportunities.
Public scorecards—such as Leapfrog Hospital Safety Grades, US News & World Report Best Hospitals, and CMS Star Ratings—calculate risk-adjusted mortality and complication rates based on secondary diagnoses. If a patient's pre-existing chronic conditions or extreme acuity are not captured, an adverse clinical outcome or inpatient death is treated by the risk model as an unexpected failure, severely penalizing your hospital's published quality rank. Proper CDI ensures your expected mortality rate accurately mirrors the true clinical risk of your patient population.
We treat physician time with clinical respect. Our queries are concise, deliver pre-filled clinical context directly inside their existing mobile or EHR in-basket, and require fewer than 30 seconds to answer. In addition, our physician liaisons run brief, high-yield coaching sessions demonstrating to clinicians how precise documentation protects their professional quality scores and reduces downstream administrative interruptions.
