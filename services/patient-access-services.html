Front-End Operational Architecture

The 6 Pillars of Modern Patient Access

Over 75% of avoidable hospital claim denials originate during initial patient scheduling and registration. Nova Arc solves this with dedicated operational pods and algorithmic checks at each stage of the pre-service workflow.

Discipline 01 • ANSI EDI 270/271

Insurance Eligibility & Benefits Verification

Automated real-time ANSI 270/271 inquiry engine validating coverage, primary/secondary coordination of benefits (COB), active deductible balances, and copay requirements before the patient arrives.

  • Instant 270/271 Batch & Real-Time Queries: Connected to over 1,200 commercial, Medicare Advantage, and state Medicaid payers.
  • Permanent Elimination of CARC 26 & 27: Identifies lapsed policies, terminated coverage, and effective date discrepancies 72 hours prior to scheduled encounters.
  • Direct EHR Workqueue Auto-Populate: Inserts detailed benefit breakdown directly into Epic Prelude/Grand Central, Cerner Soarian, and AthenaHealth.

Discipline 02 • ANSI EDI 278 & RPA

Prior Authorization Management

End-to-end prior authorization pipeline combining autonomous electronic submission (EDI 278), payer portal robotic automation, and clinical document assembly to eliminate surgical delays and CARC 197 denials.

  • Autonomous Payer Packet Assembly: Extracts physician clinical notes, diagnostic imaging reports, and CPT/HCPCS codes to compile approval-ready dossiers.
  • Sub-24-Hour Submission Window: Compresses authorization turnaround from weeks down to under 24 hours, protecting elective surgery and infusion schedules.
  • Peer-to-Peer Appeal Preparation: Certified clinical nurses organize evidence-backed appeal dossiers for attending physicians when initial denials occur.

Discipline 03 • Upfront Quality Assurance

Patient Scheduling & Registration QA

Automated demographic scrubbing, address standardization (USPS CASS), master patient index (MPI) deduplication, and photo-ID insurance card OCR to guarantee 100% accurate patient chart creation.

  • Demographic Validation & Card OCR: Extracts subscriber ID, group number, and relationship codes directly from patient insurance card scans.
  • Pre-Arrival Appointment Queue Auditing: Specialists audit all scheduled appointments 48 to 72 hours before service, eradicating Discharged Not Final Billed (DNFB) bottlenecks.
  • Master Patient Index (MPI) Deduplication: Prevents split charts and mismatched clinical records across hospital health systems.

Discipline 04 • No Surprises Act Compliance

Financial Clearance & Good Faith Estimates (GFE)

Pre-encounter patient financial clearance engine calculating accurate out-of-pocket patient liability and producing legally compliant Good Faith Estimates (GFE) under the Federal No Surprises Act.

  • No Surprises Act Statutory Delivery: Automated production and digital delivery of comprehensive GFEs within 1 to 3 business days of scheduling.
  • Contracted Payer Allowable Modeling: Applies health system fee schedules, copay tiers, and coinsurance formulas to calculate realistic cost obligations.
  • Zero-Surprise Patient Experience: Pre-cleared patients know their exact liability before admission, dramatically reducing post-care bad debt.

Discipline 06 • Clinical Order Integrity

Medical Necessity & Order Verification

Automated clinical checking against Medicare National Coverage Determinations (NCD) and Local Coverage Determinations (LCD) to ensure physician orders match billed CPT/HCPCS and ICD-10 diagnosis codes.

  • Real-Time NCD & LCD Policy Validation: Evaluates scheduled lab, imaging, and specialty orders against active CMS and commercial coverage policies.
  • Automated ABN Generation & Signature Capture: Produces compliant CMS-R-131 Advance Beneficiary Notices whenever a service is deemed non-covered.
  • Eradication of CARC 50 Denials: Prevents non-covered diagnostic write-offs by securing physician order amendments before service delivery.
