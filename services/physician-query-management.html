Clinical Communication Friction

Why Legacy Query Methods Frustrate Doctors & Stall Revenue

Overwhelming clinicians with dense, poorly formulated queries leads to ignored messages, swollen DNFB backlogs, and severe OIG audit exposure.

Physician Query Burnout & Low Response Rates

Physicians already spend up to 2 hours in the EHR for every hour of clinical care. When faced with verbose, paper-based, or portal-dependent queries with ambiguous formatting, response rates plunge below 60%. Queries get ignored, creating friction between medical staff and the HIM department.

Impact: Over 40% of clinical queries go unanswered across typical hospital systems.

Leading Query Liabilities & OIG Audit Penalties

Queries that nudge physicians toward higher-paying codes (e.g., suggesting "acute respiratory failure" without citing arterial blood gas or oxygen therapy) violate CMS, OIG, and AHIMA ethical standards. In an audit, payer clawbacks and False Claims Act penalties can wipe out millions in revenue.

Impact: Massive RAC audit recoupments and regulatory integrity monitoring.

DNFB Account Stalls & Working Capital Lockup

Every chart awaiting a physician clarification is locked in Discharged Not Final Billed (DNFB) status. When query turnaround stretches to 7 or 14 days, hospital billing cycles grind to a halt, increasing days in A/R and threatening clinical liquidity.

Impact: $2.4M+ in delayed cash flow per 200 hospital beds due to pending queries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Physician Query Management & Coaching FAQ

Operational, clinical, and regulatory guidance for Chief Medical Officers, Health Information Management directors, and hospital executive leadership.

Under the AHIMA/ACDIS Guidelines for Achieving a Compliant Query Practice, a query must never direct the provider toward a specific diagnosis, point out financial incentives, or present only one pre-determined choice. A compliant non-leading query must: (1) cite objective clinical indicators already documented in the chart; (2) present multiple clinically supported diagnostic options; and (3) include neutral options such as "Clinically Undetermined", "Other (please document)", or "Condition Not Present/Ruled Out". Nova Arc's templates are 100% compliant with these legal and ethical guardrails.
Nova Arc’s CDI specialists connect via secure VPN/VDI and operate natively inside your hospital’s existing EHR system. We utilize standard EHR clinical query functionality—such as Epic In Basket query encounters or Cerner Message Center CDI workflows. The physician receives queries in the same inbox where they sign orders and review labs, eliminating password fatigue and third-party portal adoption barriers.
We establish clear Medical Executive Committee (MEC)-approved escalation pathways. If a query remains unanswered at 48 hours post-discharge, our system generates an automated in-basket reminder. At 72 hours, a Nova Arc physician documentation liaison reaches out directly to the clinical service chief or hospitalist director. If a physician explicitly declines to clarify, the query is closed as "unanswered" and the chart is coded strictly based on existing documentation without delaying billing beyond established DNFB cutoffs.
Query fatigue is caused by volume, poor formatting, and irrelevant questions. Nova Arc eliminates low-value queries through strict pre-screening algorithms—we only query when there is clear clinical ambiguity that materially impacts acuity, patient safety indicators (PSI), or DRG grouping. In addition, our queries summarize relevant clinical data succinctly, allowing physicians to make an informed, defensible clinical choice in less than 30 seconds.
While inpatient MS-DRG and APR-DRG queries remain the foundation of hospital CDI, Nova Arc also operates robust outpatient CDI query workflows. For outpatient specialty clinics, emergency departments, and Medicare Advantage risk panels, we query for chronic condition specificity (HCC recapture) and procedural documentation completeness to secure compliant capitation RAF benchmarks and prevent outpatient claim rejections.
