Credit Balance & Refund Management

Resolve Credit Balances. Strengthen Financial Accuracy.

Connect with us
OVERVIEW

Credit balances require timely identification, validation, reconciliation, and appropriate resolution. Our Credit Balance and Refund Management services help healthcare organizations review account activity, validate credits, identify root causes, and support appropriate refund processing.

We provide structured account investigations, payment and adjustment reconciliations, and compliance monitoring to resolve credit backlogs and mitigate regulatory overpayment risk.

SERVICES PROVIDED

Systematic credit balance investigation, overpayment validation, and compliant refund disbursal.

Credit balance identification

Systematic screening and extraction of negative accounts receivable balances across patient sub-ledgers, hospital billing systems, and clinic registers.

Learn more →

Account-level investigation

Deep forensic audit of individual patient accounts to isolate transaction histories, billing timelines, and underlying balance discrepancies.

Learn more →

Payment and adjustment reconciliation

Re-matching of payer remittances, patient copays, deductibles, and contractual write-offs to correct improper posting errors and false credits.

Learn more →

Duplicate payment review

Identification of dual payments from primary and secondary insurers, erroneous multiple patient card charges, or uncoordinated payer transactions.

Learn more →

Overpayment validation

Rigorous verification verifying whether genuine payer or patient overpayments exist versus improper accounting adjustments requiring correction.

Learn more →

Refund eligibility review

Comprehensive assessment verifying rightful refund recipients, legal entitlement, absence of offset balances, and coordination of benefits (COB).

Learn more →

Refund request processing support

Preparing complete, audit-proof refund disbursement documentation packets for organizational finance and accounting department approval.

Learn more →

Credit balance resolution

Executing corrective adjustments, reallocating misapplied payments across active family accounts, or issuing statutory refunds to achieve zero balance.

Learn more →

Regulatory and payer requirement support

Strict compliance adherence supporting CMS 60-day overpayment rules, state unclaimed property (escheatment) statutes, and commercial refund guidelines.

Learn more →

Credit balance reporting

Executive-level dashboards and quarterly CMS-838 reporting tracking credit inventory, aging buckets, resolution velocity, and root causes.

Learn more →
BENEFITS

Reduce Financial Risk
