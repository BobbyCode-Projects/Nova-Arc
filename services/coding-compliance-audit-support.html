Coding Compliance & Audit Support

Strengthen Compliance. Reduce Coding Risk.

Connect with us
OVERVIEW

Coding compliance requires continuous monitoring to identify inaccuracies, inconsistencies, and potential regulatory risks. Our Coding Compliance and Audit Support services provide structured reviews of coding practices, documentation, and claims to help organizations identify opportunities for improvement.

Our audit approach focuses on accuracy, consistency, documentation support, and adherence to applicable coding and billing requirements.

SERVICES PROVIDED

Leverage independent audit expertise to safeguard compliance and revenue integrity.

Coding compliance audits

Structured baseline and periodic compliance evaluations verifying adherence to official coding guidelines and regulatory mandates.

Learn more →

Pre-bill and post-bill audits

Dual-phase auditing workflows evaluating claims prior to submission as well as retrospective analysis of paid claims.

Learn more →

Retrospective coding reviews

Statistical sampling and in-depth chart evaluations examining coding patterns, payer adjudication trends, and historical error rates.

Learn more →

Documentation validation

Rigorous validation ensuring provider documentation meets clinical necessity, billing standards, and statutory requirements.

Learn more →

Coding error identification

Systematic isolation of unbundling, upcoding, undercoding, and misapplied NCCI edits to prevent compliance inquiries.

Learn more →

Modifier and E/M audits

Focused review of high-risk modifier usage (-25, -59, -51) and Evaluation & Management level substantiation.

Learn more →

Audit sampling

Statistically valid random and targeted sampling methodologies (including RAT-STATS) ensuring reliable audit findings.

Learn more →

Compliance reporting

Clear, executive-ready audit dashboards highlighting error trends, accuracy scores, risk areas, and financial impact.

Learn more →

Corrective action recommendations

Actionable guidance and policy refinement to eliminate systemic coding defects and mitigate future audit vulnerabilities.

Learn more →

Coder education support

Targeted clinical education, coding workshops, and one-on-one coaching driven directly by audit findings and industry updates.

BENEFITS

Identify Compliance Risks Early

Regular audits help organizations identify potential coding and documentation concerns.

Improve Coding Quality

Feedback and targeted review help improve coding consistency.

Reduce Financial Exposure

Early identification of inappropriate coding can reduce potential overpayment and underpayment risks.

Support Continuous Improvement

Audit findings can be converted into actionable education and process improvements.
