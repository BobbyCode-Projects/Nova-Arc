STRATEGIC OVERVIEW

Engineered for Seamless Operations & Measurable Outcomes

Modern commerce requires deep synchronization between customer-facing storefronts, supplier purchasing, warehouse operations, and freight logistics. Fragmented systems create inventory inaccuracies, checkout delays, and fulfillment errors that erode customer trust and enterprise profitability.

Nova Arc's Digital Commerce & Supply Chain service domain provides end-to-end software engineering and system integration. We build robust B2B and B2C commerce portals, specialized FoodTech supply chain platforms, automated procurement systems, and real-time logistics tracking networks.

Our solutions connect buyers, vendors, inventory managers, and carriers into a cohesive operational ecosystem. By unifying transactional data and automating cross-system handoffs, we help organizations accelerate order fulfillment, optimize working capital, and scale commercial operations.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical guidance regarding project scope, integrations, and operational requirements.

What core capabilities are included in Digital Commerce & Supply Chain? +
The domain unifies B2B/B2C digital ordering platforms, food distribution tech, automated procurement, inventory tracking, and carrier logistics.
How do you connect digital storefronts to legacy warehouse systems? +
We build bi-directional REST, GraphQL, or EDI pipelines that sync order drops, inventory reservations, and shipping confirmations in real time.
Can contract pricing and customer credit limits be managed automatically? +
Yes. Dynamic pricing engines query negotiated account agreements and credit-line limits upon login, validating orders before ERP invoice creation.
How are split shipments and multi-warehouse allocations handled? +
Our order routing engine evaluates stock proximity and shipping costs, routing line items to optimal fulfillment hubs automatically.
What security and payment compliance standards are maintained? +
We employ tokenized checkout, TLS 1.3 encryption, PCI-compliant payment gateways, and granular role-based administrative permissions.
What is the estimated deployment timeframe for an enterprise commerce rollout? +
Initial portal architecture and core ERP integrations typically go live within 8 to 14 weeks depending on catalog complexity.
