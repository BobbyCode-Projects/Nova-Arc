FoodTech Solutions

STRATEGIC OVERVIEW

Engineered for Seamless Operations & Measurable Business Outcomes

The food and beverage industry operates under stringent regulatory compliance, perishable shelf-life constraints, variable commodity pricing, and high customer expectations for freshness and traceability.

Nova Arc builds specialized FoodTech Solutions that modernize food procurement, digital ordering, multi-kitchen inventory coordination, and distribution logistics. We engineer platforms that connect agricultural producers, food distributors, commercial kitchens, and end customers.

Our systems incorporate automated First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO) inventory algorithms, real-time temperature telemetry monitoring, batch recall tracking, and digital supplier portals, ensuring food quality, minimizing spoilage, and maximizing operational margins.

PROBLEM & RESOLUTION

Addressing Critical Operational Friction

How Nova Arc overcomes systemic process inefficiencies and technology bottlenecks.

Operational Challenge

Perishable Inventory Spoilage

Poor rotation and delayed visibility into expiry dates lead to high food waste and severe margin erosion.

Nova Arc Implementation Solution: We deploy automated FEFO inventory logic that directs warehouse pickers to oldest compliant batches first, sending automated alerts on expiring lots.
Operational Challenge

Food Safety & Recall Compliance

Locating affected products during food safety recalls using paper records takes days, increasing consumer health risks and liability.

Nova Arc Implementation Solution: Our digital batch tracking provides instantaneous farm-to-fork trace reports, identifying every recipient of a specific lot within seconds.
Operational Challenge

Fragmented Multi-Kitchen Requisitions

Individual restaurant units ordering independently lead to inefficient spot purchasing and missed volume discounts.

Nova Arc Implementation Solution: We centralize commissary and restaurant requisitions into consolidated bulk purchase orders that maximize purchasing power.
