The Systemic Failure of Siloed Point Solutions

The "Handoff Bermuda Triangle": Why Health Systems Bleed 4% to 7% of Net Revenue

In multi-hospital enterprises, revenue cycle management is routinely fragmented across 4 to 7 independent vendors and disconnected internal departments. When claims deny or aging receivables balloon, each party defends their narrow silo while the hospital CFO absorbs the financial loss.

Prior Auth Denials (CARC 197)

The Finger-Pointing: Billing blames Patient Access for failing to obtain authorization; Patient Access blames Clinic Schedulers for urgent add-ons; Schedulers blame physicians for missing clinical documentation.

Result: $2.4M average annual write-off per 500 hospital beds due to untimely authorization filing.

Clinical DRG & Downcoding Denials

The Finger-Pointing: Central Business Office blames outsourced coding vendor for downcoded DRGs; Coding vendor blames hospital CDI specialists; CDI blames physician query response delays.

Result: 0.14 Case Mix Index (CMI) degradation and millions in unrecovered clinical acuity revenue.

Aged A/R > 90 Days & Timely Filing

The Finger-Pointing: Secondary A/R agency blames primary billing team for slow initial dispatch; Billing blames payer clearinghouse; meanwhile, commercial timely-filing clocks expire.

Result: Over 22% of hospital receivables languish past 90 days, culminating in irreversible contractual write-offs.
