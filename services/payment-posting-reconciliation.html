Treasury Traps & Ledger Risk

The Silent Drain of Disjointed Cash Reconciliation

When high-volume electronic remittances, complex paper lockboxes, and multi-campus hospital deposits fall out of alignment, financial reporting fractures and patient dissatisfaction surges.

EXPOSURE 01

Dormant Unapplied Cash & Orphan EFTs

Electronic Funds Transfers (EFT) land in hospital bank accounts without matching ANSI 835 remittance files, or remittances arrive with missing account numbers. Without automated re-association, funds sit in "unapplied cash" holding suspense accounts for months, distorting month-end general ledger closes.

Financial Risk: Millions in Unposted Cash Distorting Net Revenue
EXPOSURE 02

Paper Lockbox Delays & Erroneous Patient Billing

Manual keying of multi-page paper EOBs and patient lockbox checks causes posting backlogs of 5 to 14 days. When patient deductibles or copays are not posted immediately, automated patient statement engines dispatch erroneous collection notices, triggering angry patient calls and reputational damage.

Financial Risk: Double-Billing Friction & Premature Bad Debt Placements
EXPOSURE 03

Medicare PLB Clawbacks & Credit Balance Liabilities

Medicare MAC Provider-Level Balance (PLB) adjustments—such as mandatory 2% sequestration deductions, lump-sum overpayment recoupments, and accelerated loan repayments—are often misallocated or ignored, leaving hospital general ledgers out of balance and exposing executives to CMS 60-day refund compliance penalties.

Financial Risk: OIG Audit Scrutiny & False Claims Act Credit Balance Penalties
Treasury Architecture

Comprehensive Architecture: 6 Pillars of Payment Posting

How Nova Arc achieves 99.9% posting precision through automated EDI 835 parsing, medical-grade OCR lockbox digitizing, and continuous three-way treasury balancing.

1. Automated ANSI X12 835 ERA Ingestion

Direct electronic ingestion of 835 files across Medicare MACs, state Medicaid programs, and all major commercial payers. Line-item parsing accurately applies paid amounts, contractual write-offs (CO-45), copay/coinsurance allocations (PR-1, PR-2, PR-3), and denial codes.

• Sub-Second Multi-Payer Remittance Parsing
• CAS Group Code Mapping (CO, PR, OA, PI)

2. EFT-to-ERA TRN02 Trace Re-Association

Continuous polling of hospital bank accounts matches electronic deposits (ACH/CCD+) with inbound ERA files using the TRN02 trace number. The engine prevents premature postings before cash clears while eliminating unallocated cash backlogs.

• Automated Bank Depository Trace Number Matching
• Zero Postings Prior to Certified Cash Settlement

3. Medical OCR Paper Lockbox Digitization

Converts non-electronic paper Explanation of Benefits (EOB) scans, correspondence, and patient lockbox check stubs into structured synthetic 835 files using deep-learning OCR. Human certified cash posters verify edge cases within 24 hours of bank check clearance.

• Automated Synthetic 835 Conversion
• Sub-24-Hour Paper Lockbox Turnaround

4. Three-Way Daily Treasury Balancing

Strict daily balancing covenant: Bank Lockbox Deposits = Clearinghouse ERA 835 Amounts = EHR Sub-Ledger Postings. Any penny variance generates an immediate root-cause alert, ensuring exact alignment between treasury, finance, and patient accounting.

• Daily 100% Zero-Variance Tie-Out
• Automated General Ledger Batch Export

5. Automated Secondary Crossover Trigger

Once primary remittances post, the engine evaluates remaining patient balances. If a secondary or supplemental policy is on file, the system instantly constructs an electronic secondary 837 claim embedded with primary CAS adjustments, bypassing manual billing delays.

• Instant Secondary Claim Generation
• CAS Group Code Mapping into Outbound 837

6. Credit Balance & PLB Reconciliation

Systematic management of hospital credit balances and Medicare Provider-Level Balance (PLB) adjustments. Distinguishes true overpayments from COB misallocations, automating refund documentation to meet CMS 60-day compliance windows.

• CMS 60-Day Overpayment Refund Compliance
• Medicare Sequestration & Withhold Accounting

Executive FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding EDI 835 auto-posting, paper lockbox OCR conversion, three-way treasury reconciliation, and Medicare PLBs.

Under standard ANSI X12 regulations and NACHA banking rules, health plans issue electronic remittances (EDI 835) through clearinghouses and dispatch banking deposits (ACH CCD+ format) directly to hospital treasury accounts. Both transactions carry a matching TRN02 Trace Number. Nova Arc’s cash application engine ingests the bank’s daily BAI2 or CAMT.053 electronic bank statement files and automatically pairs deposits with the corresponding 835 files based on TRN02 trace identifiers, deposit dates, and penny-exact totals. This ensures zero posting occurs before verified cash settlement.

For non-electronic payments—such as physical commercial checks, workers’ compensation vouchers, and patient personal checks sent to bank lockbox facilities—our pipeline ingests high-resolution TIFF or PDF check and voucher images directly from bank SFTP repositories. Using medical-grade Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and machine-learning document parsing, the system translates unstructured paper EOBs into structured synthetic ANSI 835 files. Certified cash posting specialists review low-confidence exceptions, guaranteeing sub-24-hour turnaround from bank deposit to EHR ledger posting.

A Provider-Level Balance (PLB) segment appears at the summary level of an 835 remittance when a payer (most notably Medicare MACs) deducts or adds funds that do not pertain to an individual patient claim. Common examples include: (1) Reason Code WO (Withhold): Mandatory federal 2% budget sequestration; (2) Reason Code FB (Forwarding Balance): Carrying negative balances forward from prior remit cycles; (3) Reason Code CS (Adjustment): Lump-sum overpayment recoupments or accelerated payment repayments. Nova Arc automatically maps each PLB reason code to designated hospital General Ledger accounts, preventing accounts receivable balancing mismatches.

Under the Affordable Care Act and False Claims Act regulations, healthcare providers must report and return identifiable Medicare and Medicaid overpayments within 60 days of identification. Unresolved credit balances represent immediate federal liability. Nova Arc systematically audits patient accounts with negative balances, determining whether the credit stems from duplicate insurance payments, primary/secondary coordination errors, or patient overpayments. Valid overpayments are routed through an automated refund workflow with complete audit documentation before statutory deadlines expire.
