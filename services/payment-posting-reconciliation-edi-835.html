Payment Posting & Reconciliation — EDI 835

Accurate Posting. Complete Reconciliation. Clear Financial Visibility.

Connect with us
OVERVIEW

Timely and accurate payment posting is essential for maintaining reliable accounts receivable information. Our Payment Posting and Reconciliation services help organizations process payer and patient payments, accurately post transactions, and reconcile financial activity.

We support electronic remittance workflows, including EDI 835 transactions, along with manual and automated posting processes.

SERVICES PROVIDED

Comprehensive remittance processing, automated cash application, and daily treasury balancing.

Electronic payment posting

Automated ingestion and processing of electronic funds transfers (EFT) directly into patient accounting systems with high first-pass match rates.

Learn more →

EDI 835 remittance processing

Systematic parsing and posting of ANSI X12 835 Electronic Remittance Advice (ERA) files, including claim adjustment reason codes (CARCs) and remittance remark codes (RARCs).

Learn more →

Manual payment posting

Precise line-item entry of paper checks, patient cash receipts, credit card transactions, and non-electronic paper explanation of benefits (EOBs).

Learn more →

ERA/EOB review

Detailed verification of electronic remittances and paper explanations of benefits to confirm contractual allowances, deductibles, copayments, and coinsurance amounts.

Learn more →

Adjustment posting

Accurate posting of contractual write-offs, courtesy discounts, administrative write-offs, and bad-debt adjustments in strict compliance with payer agreements.

Learn more →

Denial and remark code identification

Immediate capture and categorization of claim denial codes, zero-pay vouchers, and remark codes to initiate downstream denial workflows without delay.

Learn more →

Payment reconciliation

Rigorous daily three-way balancing reconciling bank deposits, merchant processing statements, and practice management posting sub-ledgers.

Learn more →

Unapplied payment management

Systematic investigation, research, and allocation of unapplied cash, unidentified electronic deposits, and suspense-account balances.

Learn more →

Variance identification

Proactive detection of underpayments, unexpected short-pays, and payer contract fee schedule variances against expected reimbursement rates.

Learn more →

Posting quality audits

Routine quality-assurance audits monitoring posting precision, balancing controls, turnaround times, and adherence to treasury standards.

Learn more →
BENEFITS

Improve Posting Accuracy

Accurate transaction posting creates a more reliable financial picture.

Strengthen A/R Visibility

Correct posting ensures that account balances reflect the latest financial activity.

Reduce Reconciliation Gaps

Systematic reconciliation helps identify payment and adjustment discrepancies.

Support Faster Follow-Up

Accurate account information allows A/R teams to prioritize unresolved balances effectively.
