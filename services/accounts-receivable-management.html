Accounts Receivable (A/R) Management

Accelerate A/R. Improve Resolution. Protect Revenue.

Connect with us
OVERVIEW

Effective A/R management requires disciplined follow-up, accurate account analysis, and timely resolution of outstanding balances. Our A/R Management services help healthcare organizations manage outstanding claims and balances across payer and patient accounts.

Our teams analyze account status, identify barriers to payment, initiate appropriate follow-up, and work toward timely resolution.

SERVICES PROVIDED

Disciplined accounts receivable follow-up to resolve aged claims and protect earned revenue.

Insurance A/R follow-up

Systematic outreach and inquiries directed to commercial, Medicare, Medicaid, and managed care payers to resolve aged, delayed, and stalled claims.

Learn more →

Patient A/R follow-up

Compassionate, professional patient communication regarding deductibles, coinsurance balances, payment options, and transparent billing inquiries.

Learn more →

Aging analysis

Comprehensive segmentation and trend analysis across 30, 60, 90, and 120+ day aging buckets to isolate revenue bottlenecks.

Learn more →

Account prioritization

Algorithmic scoring evaluating dollar thresholds, filing deadlines, payer behavior, and collection probability to target high-impact accounts first.

Learn more →

Claim status verification

Multi-channel status checks utilizing automated EDI 276/277 transactions, payer web portals, and direct representative verification.

Learn more →

Payer follow-up

Dedicated phone and portal follow-up with payer provider relations teams to expedite adjudication and resolve pending documentation requests.

Learn more →

Outstanding balance research

Deep-dive forensic review identifying root causes for non-payment, including incorrect billing data, missing attachments, or eligibility discrepancies.

Learn more →

Account documentation

Thorough, standardized note-taking within EHR and billing systems detailing all contact attempts, reference numbers, and next actionable steps.

Learn more →

Escalation management

Structured workflows escalating uncooperative payers, chronic processing delays, or high-dollar balance disputes to senior supervisory specialists.

Learn more →

A/R reporting and productivity monitoring

Executive dashboards and operational KPIs tracking Days in A/R (DAR), cash velocity, collector productivity, and resolution turnaround times.

Learn more →
BENEFITS

Improve A/R Performance
