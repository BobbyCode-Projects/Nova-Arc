SPECIALIZED CLINICAL EXPERTISE

Full-Spectrum HIM & Medical Coding Solutions

From acute inpatient facility DRG coding to multi-specialty ambulatory clinics and risk adjustment, our coders deliver unmatched quality and compliance.

Inpatient Facility & DRG Coding

Expert assignment of ICD-10-CM/PCS codes, MS-DRG and APR-DRG optimization, CC/MCC capture, and hospital-acquired condition (HAC) validation.

  • MS-DRG & APR-DRG optimization
  • CC/MCC secondary diagnosis capture
  • Length-of-stay & SOI/ROM alignment

Outpatient & Ambulatory Coding

Accurate CPT, HCPCS Level II, and modifier selection for same-day surgeries, emergency departments, radiology, pathology, and observation encounters.

  • APC reimbursement accuracy
  • Modifier -25, -59, -XU compliance
  • NCCI edit resolution

Professional Fee (ProFee) Coding

Physician specialty coding adhering to 2021/2023 E/M guidelines, procedural operative reports, and split/shared service regulations.

  • Medical decision making (MDM) scoring
  • Time-based E/M documentation
  • Multi-specialty surgical coding

Clinical Documentation Improvement

Concurrent and retrospective chart reviews identifying documentation gaps, clarifying clinical specificity, and assisting physician query creation.

  • Concurrent inpatient CDI
  • Compliant physician queries
  • Case mix index (CMI) protection

HCC & Risk Adjustment Coding

Comprehensive review of chronic condition documentation to capture accurate Hierarchical Condition Categories (HCCs) for Medicare Advantage and ACA plans.

  • MEAT criteria verification
  • Annual condition re-capture
  • RAF score revenue optimization

CAC Review & Quality Auditing

Rigorous triple-tier auditing validating Computer-Assisted Coding engine suggestions, catching missed codes, and upholding strict compliance.

  • Triple-tier coding QA
  • Pre-bill compliance reviews
  • Monthly physician education clinics
