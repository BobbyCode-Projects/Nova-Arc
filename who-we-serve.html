Industry Domain 01

FoodTech & Food Distribution

Traceability & Inventory Visibility

Industry Challenges & Operational Friction

Food distributors and food technology platforms face tight shelf-life windows, fluctuating supplier commodity prices, and multi-channel ordering demands requiring rapid turnaround.

  • Perishable Expiration Tracking: Managing batch, lot, and expiry timelines manually leads to inventory spoilage and stock write-offs.
  • Decentralized Supplier Coordination: Disconnected communication across farms, producers, and central distribution hubs delays order fulfillment.
  • Complex Multi-Tier Pricing: Balancing wholesale contract rates, volume discounts, and spot pricing across regional buyers.

How Nova Arc Delivers Solutions

Nova Arc engineers digital food commerce platforms, vendor portals, and integrated supply chain workflows that provide real-time traceability and streamline order dispatch.

  • Digital Food Ordering Platforms: B2B and B2C ordering portals with dynamic catalog management and scheduled recurring delivery cycles.
  • Batch & Expiry Tracking Systems: Automated lot tracking and FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out) dispatch rules to minimize food waste.
  • Supplier Collaboration Portals: Digital purchase requisitions, automated vendor onboarding, and purchase order confirmation workflows.
  • End-to-End Cold Chain Visibility: Integration with temperature sensors and delivery manifests to verify safety compliance in transit.

Industry Domain 02

Retail & Digital Commerce

Omnichannel Synchronization

Industry Challenges & Operational Friction

Retail brands and digital commerce businesses encounter friction synchronizing catalogs, inventory levels, order routing, and fulfillment across disparate channels.

  • Inventory Allocation Discrepancies: Overselling or stockouts caused by asynchronous inventory updates between web storefronts and warehouses.
  • Fragmented B2B Purchasing Portals: Lack of customized account pricing, credit term validation, and digital invoice generation for wholesale clients.
  • Post-Purchase Support Overhead: Repetitive customer inquiries regarding order status, tracking updates, and return approvals.

How Nova Arc Delivers Solutions

We build scalable digital storefronts, customer self-service portals, and automated fulfillment integrations that align online transactions with warehouse operations.

  • Custom B2B & B2C Storefronts: High-performance e-commerce platforms engineered for rapid browsing, mobile responsiveness, and high conversion.
  • Real-Time Inventory Synchronization: Bi-directional ERP and warehouse API connectivity ensuring accurate stock availability across all sales channels.
  • Buyer Account & Order Portals: Dedicated customer dashboards featuring purchase histories, custom pricing matrices, re-ordering, and PDF invoices.
  • Automated Fulfillment Routing: Intelligent dispatch logic routing orders to optimal fulfillment centers based on proximity and inventory depth.

Industry Domain 03

Manufacturing & Industrial Operations

IoT Monitoring & Automation

Industry Challenges & Operational Friction

Manufacturing facilities risk costly downtime and output bottlenecks when shop-floor machinery, sensor telemetry, and maintenance workflows remain isolated.

  • Unplanned Equipment Downtime: Operating without real-time telemetry means machine anomalies go unnoticed until catastrophic failure occurs.
  • Isolated Operational Technology (OT): Sensor data trapped in local PLC or SCADA controllers unable to inform enterprise production planning.
  • Manual Shift Handover Logs: Incomplete paper or spreadsheet tracking hindering operational visibility across plant managers and engineers.

How Nova Arc Delivers Solutions

We implement real-time IoT monitoring, live telemetry dashboards, and event-driven automation rules to detect threshold variances and streamline plant maintenance.

  • Edge & Cloud Telemetry Ingestion: Ingesting temperature, vibration, throughput, and power telemetry across industrial machines via MQTT/HTTP.
  • Live Plant Operational Dashboards: Unified visual control boards monitoring active production lines, cycle times, and operational KPIs.
  • Event-Driven Incident Escalation: Automated alert dispatch to maintenance personnel when equipment parameters exceed safe operating tolerances.
  • Historical Performance Analytics: Identifying chronic bottlenecks and equipment wear patterns to support data-driven preventative scheduling.
Real-Time
Centralized equipment monitoring, threshold alerts, and automated maintenance tasks.

Industry Domain 04

Logistics & Supply Chain

End-to-End Visibility

Industry Challenges & Operational Friction

Third-party logistics providers, freight operators, and supply chain coordinators struggle with multi-carrier tracking blind spots and manual dispatch handoffs.

  • Multi-Carrier Tracking Blind Spots: Disjointed carrier portals and delayed milestone updates prevent proactive exception handling.
  • Manual Dispatch & Route Inefficiencies: Static routing that fails to accommodate real-time traffic conditions, capacity limits, or delivery windows.
  • Paper-Based Proof of Delivery: Delayed physical paperwork creates billing lag and billing dispute cycles with commercial shippers.

How Nova Arc Delivers Solutions

Nova Arc develops transportation management modules, carrier API integrations, and digital proof-of-delivery systems that enhance shipment visibility and lower transit costs.

  • Unified Shipment Visibility: Centralized dashboard aggregating tracking events across ocean, air, rail, and last-mile freight providers.
  • Dispatch & Route Optimization: Configurable dispatch workflows that group orders, assign driver capacity, and sequence deliveries.
  • Digital Proof-of-Delivery (e-POD): Mobile sign-off, photo verification, and geolocation timestamps immediately accessible to billing teams.
  • Carrier Performance Analytics: Objective reporting on on-time delivery percentages, detention times, and route cost comparisons.
Visibility
Automated carrier tracking, route coordination, and digital fulfillment records.

Enterprise & Professional Services

Operational Connectivity

Industry Challenges & Operational Friction

Growing organizations frequently face administrative drag, disconnected department silos, and manual data transcription between sales, HR, and operations.

We engineer unified CRM, HRMS, and business process automation solutions that eliminate manual friction, connect departments, and support organizational scale.

  • Business Process Automation: Configurable multi-tier approval workflows, task assignment triggers, and document processing rules.

Industry Domain 06

Healthcare & Health Technology

Administrative Automation

Industry Challenges & Operational Friction

Healthcare providers and health technology companies confront intense administrative overhead, fragmented patient access workflows, and high documentation processing requirements.

  • Front-End Administrative Burden: Manual patient scheduling, registration verification, and intake errors leading to administrative backlogs.
  • Unstructured Document Ingestion: Ingesting clinical summaries, referral faxes, and medical records manually into operational systems.
  • Billing & Claim Coordination Gaps: Delayed charge reconciliation and administrative hurdles navigating complex carrier documentation guidelines.

How Nova Arc Delivers Solutions

We implement administrative automation, digital patient intake, document understanding, and operational integrations to simplify healthcare administrative workflows.

  • Digital Patient Intake & Coordination: Streamlined scheduling, intake data validation, and automated reminder notifications for clinics.
  • Administrative Document Processing: Intelligent information extraction and routing for incoming referral orders and medical paperwork.
  • Operational Healthcare Dashboards: Centralized visibility into intake volumes, administrative turnaround, and workflow throughput.
  • Administrative Billing & Claims Support: Technology-assisted validation for billing documentation, account inquiries, and system connectivity.
