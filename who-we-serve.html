Care Setting 01

Urgent Care Centers

+48% Point-of-Service Collections

Setting-Specific RCM Challenges

Urgent care clinics face intense pressures from high-volume same-day coding turnaround and self-pay collection leakage, compounded by inconsistent payer handling of S9083 global billing versus fee-for-service E/M codes.

  • Rapid Walk-In Registration: High patient velocity leads to frequent typos, invalid policy numbers, and secondary insurance omissions.
  • Complex S-Code Adjudication: Payer inconsistencies regarding S9083 global billing versus fee-for-service E/M codes cause frequent denials.
  • Self-Pay Revenue Leakage: Patients departing without settling copayments or deductibles, creating expensive bad-debt collection cycles.

How Nova Arc Solves It

Nova Arc combines same-day E/M chart coding and automated modifier -25 scrubbing with sub-second eligibility verification and card-on-file capture to prevent billing delays and eliminate walkout self-pay leakage.

  • Sub-3-Second Eligibility Checks: Automated EDI 270/271 queries instantly confirm active coverage and exact copay amounts upon scan.
  • Tokenized Card-on-File POS Workflows: Collects copays and secures pre-authorized credit cards for remaining balances, lifting collections by 48%.
  • Automated E/M Modifier -25 Scrubbing: Protects legitimate minor procedures (sutures, splinting, X-ray interpretations) from unbundling rejections.
  • Same-Day EDI Dispatch: Accelerates cash turnaround, depositing commercial carrier payments in under 12 days.

Care Setting 02

Ambulatory Surgery Centers (ASCs)

Zero Implant Carve-Out Leakage

Setting-Specific RCM Challenges

Surgery centers face severe financial risk from implant and device pass-through coding complexity, missing vendor invoice carve-outs, and high-dollar claim denials driven by multiple procedure unbundling.

  • Implant & Hardware Invoice Carve-Outs: Failure to attach manufacturer invoices or comply with specific payer markup rules results in zero implant payment.
  • Dual-Track Facility vs. Professional Discrepancies: Disconnects between the ASC facility claim (UB-04) and surgeon professional bill (CMS-1500) trigger audits.
  • Pre-Surgical Prior-Auth Stalls: Delayed carrier authorizations force procedure rescheduling, idling expensive operating rooms.

How Nova Arc Solves It

Nova Arc deploys CASCC-certified surgical coders who digitally link vendor invoices to electronic 837I claims and run automated Multiple Procedure Discount (MPD) audits to recover full implant carve-out dollars and eliminate high-dollar claim denials.

  • Automated Implant Carve-Out Matching: Digitally links vendor invoice PDFs to electronic 837I claims, complying with carrier-specific markup schedules.
  • Specialty Surgical Pods (CASCC Certified): AAPC certified coders specialized in orthopedics, ophthalmology, pain management, and gastroenterology.
  • 72-Hour Pre-Op Prior-Auth Guarantee: Proactive authorization team confirms surgical clearance 72 hours ahead of scheduled OR time.
  • Multiple Procedure Discount (MPD) Audits: Sequences CPT codes precisely to optimize primary and secondary surgical reimbursements.

Care Setting 03

Physical, Occupational Therapy & Rehab

100% 8-Minute Rule Compliance

Setting-Specific RCM Challenges

Physical therapy and rehab practices face steep revenue hurdles around CMS therapy cap tracking with mandatory KX modifier thresholds, ongoing authorization renewals before visit exhaustion, and unit-math errors under the 8-minute rule.

  • CMS 8-Minute Rule Calculation Errors: Inaccurate unit allocation across timed CPT codes (97110 therapeutic exercise, 97140 manual therapy) triggers clawbacks.
  • Authorized Visit Exhaustion: Treating patients beyond approved insurance visit allowances results in uncollectible retroactive denial write-offs.
  • Unsigned Plan of Care (POC) Recertifications: Failing to secure referring physician signatures within 30 days causes technical Medicare claim rejections.

How Nova Arc Solves It

Nova Arc automates 8-minute rule calculations and deploys a proactive visit countdown radar that flags therapy caps and triggers timely KX modifier attachments and re-authorizations before care is disrupted.

  • Automated 8-Minute Rule Verification: Mathematical pre-bill validation ensures total treatment time matches billed CPT units exactly.
  • Proactive Visit Countdown Radar: Alerts clinical coordinators when a patient has 2 authorized visits remaining, prompting timely re-auth requests.
  • Medicare KX Modifier & Cap Automation: Applies KX modifiers automatically upon reaching annual statutory therapy thresholds with audit-proof documentation.
  • Physician POC Tracking & E-Fax Escalation: Tracks plan-of-care signature turnaround, triggering automatic physician reminders before 30-day deadlines.

Care Setting 04

Behavioral Health & Substance Use

99.2% Parity Claim Acceptance

Setting-Specific RCM Challenges

Behavioral health providers face unique revenue vulnerabilities around session-limit authorizations, complex mental health parity law billing nuances, and disjointed third-party carve-out payer clearinghouses.

  • Third-Party Behavioral Carve-Out Gateways: Commercial medical plans frequently route mental health claims to third-party clearinghouse IDs (e.g. Optum, Beacon, Magellan), causing incorrect clearinghouse rejections.
  • Psychotherapy Add-On Coding Complexity: Complex duration rules for add-on psychotherapy codes (+90833, +90836, +90838) billed alongside medical E/M evaluations.
  • Telehealth Place of Service (POS) Rule Shifts: Constant shifts between POS 02 (telehealth outside patient home) and POS 10 (patient home) causing carrier denials.

How Nova Arc Solves It

Nova Arc automatically identifies behavioral carve-out payer gateways during pre-registration and enforces clinician license-aware billing rules to track authorized session intervals and maintain full parity compliance.

  • Automated Carve-Out Payer Routing: Identifies underlying mental health clearinghouse routing codes automatically from patient 270 eligibility checks.
  • E/M & Psychotherapy Combination Scrubbing: Validates clinical time documentation to ensure compliant pairing of psychiatric evaluations and therapy sessions.
  • Telehealth POS 02/10 & Modifier Validation: Auto-applies modifiers (-95, -GT) based on specific state Medicaid and commercial telehealth policies.
  • Sensitive Patient Financial Advocacy: Compassionate, discrete digital patient billing statements with confidential payment plan setups.
