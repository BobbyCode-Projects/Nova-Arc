OVERVIEW

Strengthening Patient Access from the Very Beginning

Every successful patient encounter starts with accurate and complete information. Our Patient Access solutions help healthcare organizations verify patient demographics, confirm insurance coverage, and identify missing or inconsistent information before the visit.

Our experienced team proactively reviews eligibility, benefits, insurance details, and registration information to identify potential issues at the front end. By resolving discrepancies before they move downstream, we help organizations minimize avoidable delays, reduce front-end denial risk, and create a smoother patient experience.

Through a structured and proactive approach, we help strengthen the point of access while supporting greater efficiency across the revenue cycle.

PROCESS & PROCEDURE

Generate savings with efficient RCM, medical billing, and coding

Our comprehensive approach to understanding and streamlining your revenue cycle means you can stop worrying about revenue and keep your attention on patients.

01

Patient Access & Pre-Service Validation

We begin by establishing accurate patient and insurance information through eligibility verification, benefits validation, scheduling and registration QA, prior authorization, financial clearance, and medical necessity checks.

02

Documentation, Coding & Charge Validation

Clinical documentation and encounter information are reviewed to support accurate medical coding, charge capture, HCC/risk adjustment, facility/DRG, and professional fee coding while identifying documentation gaps.

03

Coding Quality & Compliance Review

Our team performs coding QA, CDI review, CAC review, physician query management, and compliance audits to help ensure coding accuracy, consistency, and appropriate reimbursement.

04

Claims Submission & Payment Processing

Clean claims are prepared, validated, scrubbed, and submitted electronically. Once payments are received, payment posting and reconciliation are performed to maintain accurate account balances and financial records.

05

A/R, Denial & Underpayment Management

Outstanding accounts are monitored and analyzed to identify denials, underpayments, payer issues, and aging balances. Appropriate follow-up, appeals, recovery actions, and contract compliance reviews are performed.

06

Patient Financial Services & Revenue Recovery

We support patient billing, self-pay collections, credit balance and refund management, EDI enrollment, and other recovery activities to help organizations optimize the full revenue cycle.
