FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical guidance regarding project scope, integrations, and operational requirements.

What information should we prepare for our initial project discovery call? +
Helpful details include an overview of your primary operational bottlenecks, the existing software tools you currently use, desired business outcomes, and your target timeline for delivery.
Do you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) before discussing our project? +
Yes. We execute mutual NDAs prior to any detailed technical discussion to ensure your proprietary business workflows, architecture, and confidential data remain fully protected.
What engagement models does Nova Arc offer for project delivery? +
We offer flexible models tailored to client needs: fixed-scope milestone deliverables with clear requirements, or dedicated engineering squads providing agile sprint-based development.
Who owns the source code and intellectual property developed during the project? +
All custom software code, database schemas, architectural artifacts, and intellectual property developed by Nova Arc become 100% client property upon milestone completion.
How do you manage project scope changes during development? +
We manage changes through formal agile sprint planning. Any new requirement is scoped, estimated for effort and timeline impact, and prioritized collaboratively before implementation begins.
What level of technical support is provided after the project goes live? +
Every deployment includes a standard warranty period covering defect remediation, followed by optional SLA-backed ongoing maintenance agreements for security updates and continuous enhancements.
