Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers on response timelines, data security, EHR integrations, and complimentary revenue cycle evaluations.

How quickly will I hear back after submitting?

All inquiries are assigned directly to an RCM architect in under 2 business hours. If you submit during weekends or observed holidays, our on-call triage team monitors critical requests.

Can we sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) upfront?

Yes. Nova Arc provides standard mutual NDAs and HIPAA-compliant BAAs immediately prior to any clinical data or billing remittance evaluations, ensuring end-to-end statutory compliance.

What is needed for a complimentary A/R leak analysis?

Our audit requires zero invasive IT deployment: simply export 90 to 180 days of historical 835 remittance data or an aged trial balance from Epic, Cerner, AthenaHealth, or eClinicalWorks.

Do we get dedicated account representatives?

Every client partner is paired with a dedicated U.S.-based Client Engagement Director and a pod of certified AAPC/AHIMA specialists, ensuring personal accountability with weekly cadence reviews.
