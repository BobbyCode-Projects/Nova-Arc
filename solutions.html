Deep Dive

Patient Access & Front-End Integrity

Front-end registration and eligibility errors account for over 46% of all downstream claim denials in US healthcare systems. Nova Arc solves this by deploying automated robotic verification tools directly into your patient intake workflow.

Key Capabilities & Architecture:

  • Real-Time 270/271 EDI Integration: Instant querying across 1,200+ payers for co-pay, deductible remaining, coinsurance, and service-specific exclusions.
  • Automated Prior-Authorization: RPA bots ingest physician orders from Epic/Cerner and initiate portal authorizations before appointment check-in.
  • Good Faith Estimate Calculator: Fully compliant with the federal No Surprises Act, producing accurate pre-treatment price estimates in seconds.
  • Pre-Service Digital Capture: Secure SMS text-to-pay and contactless patient check-in increasing upfront self-pay collections by over 45%.

Health Information Management & Coding

Nova Arc pairs AAPC and AHIMA credentialed coding teams with AI-assisted chart auditing to ensure every clinical service rendered is compliantly coded and fully reimbursed.

Key Capabilities & Architecture:

  • Sub-24-Hour Chart Turnaround: Dedicated specialty pods ensure encounter charts are coded and audited within 24 hours of clinical discharge.
  • Clinical Documentation Improvement (CDI): Proactive physician querying and documentation coaching to capture true patient acuity and elevate CMI.
  • Hierarchical Condition Category (HCC) Capture: Risk adjustment optimization for Medicare Advantage and value-based care contracts.
  • Dual-Layer Quality Auditing: Regular peer reviews and randomized algorithmic audits ensuring a consistent 98.6%+ coding quality rate.
